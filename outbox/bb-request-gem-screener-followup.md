One tooltip style everywhere in the app (the card of A.3), including tiles, the Revenue tile, table cells and the Legend. No browser-native tooltips left.

Chains tab: drop the share from the Layer, Share, Theme cell (Apps keep theirs).
Chains are priced on adoption, Apps on revenue. Design the best algorithm you can to show a chain's TRUE adoption (method and data are yours): say how it resists wash trading and incentives, and how chains with very low fees are treated.
