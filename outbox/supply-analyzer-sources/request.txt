Follow-up to ET-31267: upgrade the live Supply Analyzer page and the Supply column in the Gem Screener themselves, not new pages beside them; they keep their names, logos and copy, and the template lines about rebuilding someone else's app do not apply. Goals, not methods: how you build it is your call.
Why: 143 of the 236 apps read n/a in the Supply column, 4 of the 10 Degen picks among them, because the only source for a future schedule is DeFiLlama (our own line in ET-31267, which this replaces). Collector Crypt, the first pick, reads "dilution unknown" and passes the gate, while its public Streamflow dashboard shows 346.8M CARDS still locked in vesting, on a schedule to July 2027, against 885M circulating: about +39% in 12 months, over the gate's 25%.
MUST STAY AS DRAWN: the columns in picture 1 and the pie in pictures 2 (coin panel) and 3 (Supply Analyzer page), in the site's fonts and palette.

1. Where DeFiLlama has no schedule, the Supply Analyzer finds it in the other public places: the project's own vesting contracts (Streamflow and the like), Tokenomist, DropsTab. "One source per number" stays: each coin's schedule comes from one source, and DeFiLlama stays first.
2. The Supply card's Next unlock row, the Supply Analyzer page and the column's tooltip name that source and when it was read.
3. The Dilution gate reads these figures with no change to its rule, so a Degen pick can leave because of one. Collector Crypt is the case we know of. Why it left shows on its near-miss card and in the row, never beside the Top Picks heading, which stays as it is.
4. The Supply column shows its number alone, with a grey "n/a" where there is none; its pie moves to the Market Cap column (item 11). Today's full cyan pie says "100% free to sell" on rows whose vesting and staking are unknown; a pie draws only what is known.
5. Supply column, max supply reached (circulating within 1% of max): "0%" in green, with the tooltip "Max supply is reached: no more tokens can come out."
6. Supply column, a schedule that has finished: never "No vesting or unlock schedule on any feed we read". If it covered the whole supply, "0%" (Tornado Cash). If not, it stays n/a and the tooltip gives the date and the share that never had a schedule (Jupiter: finished February 2026, 50.6% of supply has none).
7. Supply column, nothing found anywhere: the tooltip names where it looked: "No schedule found on DeFiLlama, the project's vesting contracts, Tokenomist or DropsTab. Nothing is assumed."
8. Lombard reads its schedule. DeFiLlama holds it to 2029, about +67% in 12 months, and the row reads "ok" and "No unlock schedule on file".
9. The column is renamed "Dilution 12M" on Apps and Chains, because that is what its number is: new tokens in the next 12 months over circulating. Its (i) says so. The Supply card in the coin panel keeps its name.
10. Coin panel, Supply card and the Supply Analyzer page: the three tiles Circulating, Market cap to FDV and Dilution 12 months become one pie of the whole supply, as in picture 2, because they are one picture: market cap over FDV is circulating over the supply, and dilution is what is coming over what is out. To holders and Staked stay as tiles. Its parts: in circulation (split into free to sell and staked where staking is known), coming in 12 months, after that or never issued; with no schedule the part not out yet is dashed; with no max supply only what is out is drawn, and it says so. The centre gives the share that is out. Beside it, each part's amount, then market cap, FDV with the multiple, and Dilution 12 months, with today's red and green rules.
11. Market Cap column on Apps and Chains: the figure stays, with the same pie small above it. Its tooltip gives the share that is out and the FDV with the multiple.
