## 1. Chains: which chains are listed
Goal: the Chains tab lists the L1 and L2 chains a degen would look for. The list is every chain whose native token is in CoinGecko's "Layer 1" or "Layer 2" category above a market-cap cut; each is joined by `gecko_id` to DefiLlama's chain list (the data behind defillama.com/chains) for TVL, users and stablecoins. A chain with no DefiLlama row or no such data stays listed and shows "no data"; fees above a floor are not a condition for being listed.

Of CoinGecko's top 40 Layer 1 coins, 17 have a DefiLlama chain row and are not on the tab: BTC, XRP, ZEC, ADA, BCH, LTC, CC, HBAR, TAO, CRO, ETC, ALGO, STABLE, VET, XDC, FLR, SEI. Seven more have no DefiLlama row (XMR, M, KAS, PI, NIGHT, BDX, TIA): they would show "no data".
- IOTA is rank 49 of CoinGecko's Layer 1 ($258M) and has a DefiLlama row (TVL $2.0M, `gecko_id` iota, plus "IOTA EVM", $0.7M); it is not on the tab. Sui is rank 17 and is on the tab.

## 3. Chains: real adoption, the benchmark and its badge
Goal: a chain's adoption, its price-tag line and its Upside do not depend on what a transaction costs there, TVL, volume and active users all count, and no single input can be inflated cheaply to lift them.

Why it changes. Today's adoption is the geometric mean of the chain's yearly fees and its daily users valued at a reference fee: sqrt(fees × users × 1.2016 × 365), recomputed on Avalanche, Polygon, Aptos, Ethereum, Starknet, Solana and Sui and equal to the page's `adoption.value` to six digits (where the page's own guards discount a chain's fees, as on Tron, the page's value is lower). Fees carry half the weight: at the same users, a chain with fees 10× cheaper gets about 3.2× less adoption. Ethereum has about 4× Sui's users (520K against 131K a day) and about 15× its adoption.

### 3a. The measure (a good default; the builder may improve it and say what changed and why)
- **Inputs per chain, from DefiLlama.** U = active addresses a day, the lower of the 30-day and the 90-day median (a campaign spike counts only once it lasts). V = DEX volume over 30 days. T = TVL without the chain's own token and without double counting (TVL in its own token rises with the price it is meant to judge). S = stablecoins on the chain.
- **Cheap signals count only as far as expensive ones back them.** Addresses and volume are cheap to fake (bots, airdrop farming, wash trading), cheapest on cheap chains; TVL and stablecoins cost real money. U counts at most up to the 80th percentile, across the listed chains, of users per dollar of T or of users per dollar of S, whichever backs more (a payments chain such as Tron or Stellar holds its money in stablecoins, not in DeFi). V counts at most up to the 80th percentile of 30-day turnover V/T.
- **Adoption = (U × V × T × S)^(1/4)**, with the capped U and V. A geometric mean: no input carries a chain on its own.
- **No fees** in it: a fee in dollars is gas times the token's price, so it rises with the price it is compared with, and it marks cheap chains down. Fees may stay as a column.
- **No Metcalfe** (users squared): it squares fake addresses, and across today's chains market cap grows with users at a power of about 0.4, not 2.
- **A missing input** shows as missing; that chain gets no adoption figure and no Upside, rather than one made from three inputs.

### 3b. The benchmark and Upside
- **Multiple** = market cap ÷ adoption. **Upside** = the benchmark multiple ÷ the chain's multiple (the same form as today).
- **Benchmark = the median multiple of the eligible large chains**: a year of data or more, market cap $1B or more, all four inputs fresh, and the caps cut at most half of the chain's users or volume. Today that is 14 chains (Avalanche, Polygon, Ethereum, Stellar, Solana, Sui, TON, Tron, ICP, Hyperliquid L1, Mantle, Arbitrum, BSC, X Layer) and a median of **60×**.
- **Not one leader.** Under today's rule ("most adoption") the leader becomes Ethereum at 90×. ETH's price carries its role as money, so as the yardstick it makes 14 of 22 chains look cheaper, Polygon by 20×; and one chain re-pricing would move every Upside on the tab. A median of 14 does neither.

### 3d. Tag: the token does not collect the chain's fees
- Where gas is paid in another token, by DefiLlama's own fields (`gasTokenGeckoId` is not the chain's `gecko_id`), the row and the detail carry a tag: "Gas is paid in ETH: this token does not collect the chain's fees." A tag, not a gate, like Apps' "Reaches you". Today: Arbitrum, OP Mainnet, Linea, ZKsync Era. Two of them are among the largest Upsides below.

### 3e. What it gives today (the page's own data, the 22 chains with all four inputs; TVL there still includes the chain's own token, which the page does not split)
- Adoption order: Ethereum, Solana, BSC, Tron, Polygon, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Hyperliquid L1, Sui, Monad, X Layer, Plasma, OP Mainnet, Stellar, Near, TON, Aptos, Mantle, Starknet, Linea, ICP, Filecoin.
- Upside against 60×: Polygon 13.6, Monad 13.2, Arbitrum 10.9 (gas in ETH), OP Mainnet 8.8 (gas in ETH), Plasma 7.3, Tron 2.5, Aptos 2.5, Linea 2.1 (gas in ETH), Avalanche 2.0, Starknet 1.9, Solana 1.5, X Layer 1.4, Sui 1.1; below 1: BSC 0.9, Ethereum 0.7, TON 0.4, Hyperliquid L1 0.5, Near 0.3, Stellar 0.3, Mantle 0.3, ICP and Filecoin about 0. 13 of 22 above 1; the page today has 5, with Polygon at 5.6.
- The caps cut volume on Near (−76 %), Solana (−48 %), Polygon (−32 %), Hyperliquid L1 (−27 %), and users on Filecoin (−6 %) and TON (−4 %); nothing else moves.
- Faking 10× the users and 10× the DEX volume lifts adoption ×1.2 on Near and Solana, ×1.5 on TON, ×1.6 on Sui, ×2.2 on Monad, ×2.85 on Tron (its stablecoins back many users); without the caps ×3.2; today's measure ×3.2, or ×10 if the fake transactions also pay fees.
- Log-log R² against market cap: this measure 0.50; TVL alone 0.47; DEX volume 0.41; stablecoins 0.39; users 0.34; fees alone 0.27; today's measure 0.59, partly because fees and own-token TVL move with the token's price.
- Tried and set aside: capping users by both kinds of money at once (cut users that look real: Tron −25 %, Sui −39 %, Aptos −43 %); the 90th percentile (lets fakes through: Tron ×3.16); Ethereum as the leader (above); a fair-value line fitted across chains instead of one multiple (it halves the small chains' Upside, Polygon 6.8, but its slope moves with every refresh on about 22 points).

### 3f. Optional, only if Cymetica already has such a source (not worth buying or building for this tab)
Count as users the addresses that come back week after week, or the wallets holding at least $10 in stablecoins: a million fake funded wallets cost $10M parked, a million fake addresses almost nothing on a cheap chain. Checked 2026-10-01: Artemis has returning users and stablecoin active addresses per chain (stablecoin tables for about 18 large chains), API from $500 a month (the "Plugin" plan) or from $50k a year; RWA.xyz has stablecoin holders on 39 networks, API on an enterprise plan; Visa's Onchain Analytics (Allium) has bot-filtered stablecoin activity on 10 chains as a free dashboard without an API; Dune has the raw data, as custom SQL per chain. DefiLlama has none of them.
