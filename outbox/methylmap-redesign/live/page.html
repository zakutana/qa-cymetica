MethylMap

A 13-gene methylation report from the raw DNA file you already have. Your file is read here in your browser and is never uploaded. Every line comes with its source.

Built by Cymetica Biosciences with Health Builder

Information only, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before changing anything.

Start

See a made-up demo first, or read your own file. Nothing leaves this device.

Type genotypes by hand

Copy each genotype from your report as two letters, for example AG. Write them as your raw-data file or lab report shows them; a genotype written on the other DNA strand is turned round for you. Leave a box empty if your report does not have it. Reading a PDF or a photo of a lab report is not supported yet, so typing is the way to use one.

Get your raw DNA file

If you have not taken a DNA test, any of the consumer tests below gives a raw file that works here; they sell kits in many countries. If you already tested, download the file from your account:

Your privacy, in plain words

  • Your DNA file stays on your device. It is read by this page in your browser. It is not uploaded, not sent to us and not sent to any AI model. Only the methylation markers in the table below are kept in memory.
  • Research lookups use gene names only. When you open a gene, this page asks our research service for papers about that gene by name (for example "MTHFR"). Your genotype is not in the question.
  • Ask BioAI is your choice. The Ask BioAI button opens our research chat with a question that names your genes and results in words (no raw genotypes). Nothing is sent until you press it.
  • Saving is optional and encrypted. If you press Save, your markers, answers, blood results and check-ins are encrypted (AES-GCM, with a key made by and kept inside this browser) and stored on this device only. Anyone who can use this browser profile can open the page and see them, so do not save on a shared computer.
  • Export and delete in one click. Download everything as JSON or CSV, or delete everything this page stored.
  • No third-party analytics or ad trackers run on this site.
  • We make no HIPAA, GDPR or other certification claim for this app. The site's full Privacy Policy applies.

What MethylMap does not do

  • It does not diagnose anything. A variant is a tendency, not a disease.
  • It gives no doses and no prescriptions.
  • It cannot tell your biological age: that needs an epigenetic (DNA methylation) test of a blood or saliva sample, not a genotype file.
  • It does not read a PDF or a photo of a lab report yet: type the genotypes from the report instead.
  • It reads only the 22 markers in the table below, not your whole genome.
