Promo Market v3: changes on top of v2 (ET-32929). Its own address, any website as a brief, every price with its unit, a post priced for each promoter by their real reach on X with the owner's approval, a trimmed brief page, and a user-protection line on the market. Build spec: https://github.com/zakutana/qa-cymetica/blob/1cee6f7ef8ca2bbd091ce48abb68013df57a9284/outbox/promo-market-v3/SPEC.md . Pictures: https://github.com/zakutana/qa-cymetica/tree/1cee6f7ef8ca2bbd091ce48abb68013df57a9284/outbox/promo-market-v3/build-it-better/pictures ("picture N" below means N-*.webp), taken at 2000 px wide from your own live v2 page with these changes on it.
We give only the look; how it works is yours. The look is binding: every changed screen must look like its picture. How reach is measured, how fakes are caught and every word the page says about protection are yours to decide, write and secure. Where your backend cannot do what is drawn, change as little as it takes and name each change in the BB-SPEC. Everything not named here stays as v2 has it.
The look is code on top of your own file, so nothing needs redrawing: live/v3.js goes at the end of the closure in your promo_market_v2.js (before route(false); bootStart();) and its functions replace yours of the same name (chrome, defaultForm, formBrief, briefCard, newBrief, ownerBrief) calling your helpers as they are; live/v3.css loads after promo_market_v2.css; live/patch.py JS_ANCHORS lists the five small text changes in your file; live/sample.py is the sample brief the pictures show (invented, handles ending _demo). All in the same folder as the spec.
1. cymetica.com/promo-market opens the Market tab for anybody, signed in or not: it is the product's own address. /creator-dashboard (the daily Telegram link) keeps opening a member's dashboard as today.
2. The Universe says Made by BJ Ironstar Capital on the Promo Market building and its panel (today: Cymetica).
3. Post a brief: the first tile of the product picker is My own website (a name and a URL), so an owner can promote any site, also one outside Cymetica; it is also the default when the owner has no products. Per new user is off for such a brief until the site can report a sign-up to you (pictures 1, 2).
4. Every price says its unit: each price field ends in USDC with its value under it (= 5 ¢, = $5), one line above the prices says all prices are in USDC (1 USDC = $1), and the brief's card says the same.
5. Cover picture in place of Picture for posts: the picture that goes with every promoter's post, by default the product page's shot, replaced by the first picture the owner drops; shown 132 x 74, its line wraps (picture 1).
6. The link field in Materials is full width (today it is the width of the icon column and shows https:/).
7. Per new user says the rule that counts: spends at least this on Cymetica in 14 days, a deposit alone never counts. Today the label says own deposit and only the tooltip says your fees rule; the brief's drawer says the same.
8. Per unique visitor comes first in the pay rows, then Per post, which is priced from it.
9. Per post has no price to type: the owner only switches it on (marked auto). The post is paid for itself, whatever it brings (picture 1).
10. On the market a brief's card shows by reach / per post / for your X and its button is See my offer. In the brief the promoter types their X handle and presses Check my offer (pictures 5, 6).
11. The promoter then sees followers, views a post, real audience, account age, and Your offer for one post in large type: the unique visitors you expect from one post on that account x the owner's price per visitor, rounded so it reads well (halves under 10 USDC, whole USDC to 100, fives above), e.g. ~136 visitors x 0.05 = 6.80 -> 7 USDC; then Take the offer (picture 7). An account you refuse (bought audience, new account, one device or wallet behind two promoters, or whatever you judge) sees Not eligible and why (picture 8).
12. The post is paid once it has stayed up 7 days, as today. The visitors it brings count first against what the post was paid; above that each is paid at the visitor price, so the owner never pays twice for the same people.
13. The owner approves each offer; the promoter's link arrives on approval.
14. A brief's own page (#mybrief/<id>, picture 3): four numbers only, Spent, Up for grabs, Visitors (unique, 14 days), New users. Not shown: New visitors (a second count of the same people) and Posts paid.
15. Offers for a post, right under the numbers: only the promoters waiting for the owner, with followers, views a post, real audience, visitors expected and the offer, and Approve or an x to decline. N filtered out by Cymetica beside the title, the reasons on hover. An approved one moves to Promoters. It replaces Quotes from promoters.
16. Visitors by day shows on hover over each day's column the day, its visitors and its new users (picture 4).
17. One Promoters table, full width: promoter (X handle), the post (its price and one state: approved, posted, paid), visitors, new users, earned, Pay. Today's table cuts its last column (paid / Pay) at the card's edge.
18. Not shown any more on the brief's page: Where they came from, Not counted, Materials; and no N materials tag on the brief's card. Promoters still see the materials inside the brief.
19. A bell in the top bar left of live / read: the number of offers waiting across the owner's briefs; a click opens the brief that has them (picture 3).
20. One line above Open briefs on the market: a shield, User protection, a short line, four pills and How, which opens the fold at the bottom (picture 5). Only the place and the look are ours: the words in the pictures are placeholders; you decide what the protection is, write the line, the pills and the fold from it, and secure it.
