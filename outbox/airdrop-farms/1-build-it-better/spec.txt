Rebuild of Airdrop Farmer (https://cymetica.com/airdrop-farmer). Pictures 1-5 are the target: layout, parts and look as drawn, in the site's fonts and palette. Show only what is drawn. Farm names and figures in the pictures are examples.
Why: the live build misses the idea. It farms nothing anyone is going to airdrop: 7 of its 8 programs are chains whose token is already live (ETH, ARB, OP, HYPE, BNB, AVAX, POL), the 8th is LI.FI routing. It only bridges and swaps stablecoins "on a jittered human cadence", the pattern airdrop programs filter out as sybil, and every farm wallet is created, funded and driven by Cymetica on the same routes, so they can read as one cluster. Its fee budget (/api/v1/airdrop-farm/config: 0.35% of the deployment a day on Balanced, 1% on Degen) lets fees take up to 63% of a deployment in 6 months on Balanced and all of it in 100 days on Degen, and deployments are now open. The user cannot pick farms or amounts and sees no estimate.
The idea: one amount of USDC, split across the real points programs, testnets and task farms of protocols that have no token yet, and a live view of what each farms. Keep from the live build: one farm wallet per person, never pooled; every step a real transaction; stop any time back to USDC; the 10% of realized profit fee.

Header (pictures 1, 4): the logo of picture 5 (an AI agent's round head with two green eyes under a farmer's wide-brim hat, a sprout growing from the crown, cyan to green), "AIRDROP" small over "FARMER", tabs Farms and Live farms as one segmented control (active one green, Live farms with its count), and at the right "FARMING · 10 farms · $5,000 in" or "NOT FARMING".

Cymetica farms them."; the plan panel: "HOW MUCH USDC?" as one big input with a USDC pill and MAX, the balance, "YOU FARM ≈ $900 – $3,890" big and green (counts to each new value), "in airdrops over 6 months · 10 farms · $5,000 planted", a big green START FARMING, top right "BEST SPLIT ✓" or "↺ BEST SPLIT" after a hand change.
3. Right of it, the farm from above: an isometric field of plots, one pillar of light per farm switched on, as tall as the USDC in it (square root, so small farms still show), the farm's own logo on top, the tallest at the back; free farms are short cyan pillars; up to 16 plots, then "+N more farms". It redraws as soon as an amount changes.
4. Typing the amount fills in its best split at once. Any farm takes any amount up to the whole balance: changing a farm changes the total, and once all the USDC is in farms, what is added to one comes out of the farms that farm least (a note says how much, from how many).
5. "Every farm, best first" with a search, then one ranked list: rank (top 3 green), logo, name with ONE chip (TOKEN CONFIRMED, TGE Q4 2026, POINTS → INK, POINTS LIVE, PAYS DAILY, PAYS WEEKLY, FREE, SPECULATIVE), PLANT (a thick glowing slider on a square-root scale from 0 to the whole balance, a cyan BEST notch at the best amount), the USDC field (two-way with the slider; "best $700" under it only when off the best amount, a click sets it), the est. airdrop big and green, a toggle. Order: what a dollar farms there net of costs, then free farms, then speculative ones; a farm with nothing to farm now is not listed. A free farm shows FREE; a farm switched off is dimmed and shows in grey what its best amount would farm. The estimate moves under the finger while dragging.
6. Honest estimates: est. airdrop = points per week x weeks x the user's share of all points x the points pool x the token value at launch (FDV range), plus what the farm pays out on the way; a bigger wallet gets less per dollar; always a range; "no estimate" when a farm publishes no points rate or pool.
7. The best split: a first ticket of 5% of the amount, then a step at a time to the farm where the next dollar earns most net of costs and gas, at most 20% of the amount in one farm, none without an estimate.
8. The list is live and complete: every protocol without a token that runs a points program or has said a token is coming (DefiLlama's list of protocols without a token, and each project's own points page), plus testnets and task farms, each with its rules read from its own page. A farm that ends or launches its token leaves the list within a day (for users in it, it turns CLAIMABLE).
9. Start farming (picture 3): one confirmation: farms and amount, est. airdrops big, "See the 10 farms" folded, est. cost (fees, funding, gas) and the fee. Then the agent does everything: deposits, bridges, LP quotes, trades, testnet transactions and tasks, points sync, claims. Trading farms are hedged where possible (a long on one venue against a short on another). Never wash trades or self-trades.
10. One wallet, the user's own USDC: never more wallets per user, never activity for its own sake, nothing a farm's terms forbid; a farm whose terms exclude automated participation is not listed. Projects cancel sybil farms; this keeps the airdrops.

Right: the same field, each pillar as tall as what its farm has earned; a farm with something to claim glows with a HARVEST tag.

13. "Your farms", one row per farm: logo, name, one status chip (FARMING, CLAIMABLE, TGE IN 7 D, CAP 81% USED, EPOCH ENDS IN 5 D, NO POINTS YET, CLAIMED), IN, a bar of how far into its 6 months it is with its points and today's gain, earned so far big and green, a green Harvest with the amount when claimable, a quiet Stop. Stop claims what is claimable, unwinds the farm and returns the USDC.
14. Money moves into outside protocols, so this is for your human-reviewed slot; until it passes, Start farming runs the plan on paper, marked PAPER.
15. Agents: llms.txt, the agent card, MCP and /api-docs list the farms with their estimates for an amount, a plan the owner approves with one link, the live farms.
