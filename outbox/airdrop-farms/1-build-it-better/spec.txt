New app: Airdrop Farms (cymetica.com/airdrop-farms). The user types how much USDC to farm with, switches farms on in one long list of every airdrop farm in crypto, sets an amount per farm or takes the recommended one, presses Start farming, and Cymetica does the rest; a second tab shows the live farms and what each has farmed. Pictures 1-4 are the target: layout, parts and look as drawn, in the site's fonts and palette, in the Agent Tournaments shell. Farm names and figures in the pictures are examples.
Why it is a hit: this week a top crypto YouTuber said the big AI models refused to build him an airdrop farmer, so he is setting up his own local models for it. The big models refuse multi-wallet (sybil) farming, and a chatbot cannot deposit, trade or claim for anyone anyway. So no AI serves the degen who just wants their USDC farming. Cymetica can, on its own agent and models: the legitimate version, one wallet, the user's own USDC, every farm's rules kept, run end to end.
1. Header (pictures 1, 3): the logo of picture 4 (an AI agent's round head with two green eyes under a farmer's wide-brim hat, a sprout growing from the crown, cyan to green), "AIRDROP" small over "FARMS", two tabs Farms and Live farms (with the number of live farms), and at the right a live status "FARMING · 12 farms · $4,850 in" (or "Not farming").
2. Farms tab, top left, the slogan in three lines: "Deploy USDC. / Farm every airdrop. / Cymetica does the rest." (the last line green), above it a pill "412 FARMS · ONE DEPOSIT" (the live count), under it "Chatbots explain airdrops. Cymetica farms them."
3. Top right, the plan card: "USDC to farm" as one big input, the balance with 25% 50% 75% Max, a bar "Allocated $4,850 of $5,000 / $150 left", three figures (farms on, est. airdrops over the horizon as a range, est. cost), and two buttons: "Use recommended" (sets every farm to its recommended amount for this budget) and a green "Start farming". The amounts can never add up to more than the budget.
4. Under it "All farms" with the total, chips per category with counts (Perp DEX, Prediction, Lending, LP vaults, Chains, Testnets · free, Tasks · free), a sort (est. airdrop per $, recommended, newest, ending soon) and a search.
5. One row per farm: a toggle · logo, name, category · How (Deposit, Trade, LP, Bridge, Testnet, Tasks) · Token (confirmed, no token yet, points to token) with its season or deadline · a slider from 0 to the budget with the recommended amount marked by a cyan tick · the USDC field, two-way with the slider · Recommended with a "use" button (a tick when it matches) · Est. airdrop over the horizon as a range, the cost under it (fees, funding, gas). Free farms show FREE and no slider; a farm switched off is dimmed at 0.
6. Simulator (picture 2): the row's arrow opens it. A chart of USDC deployed (0 to the budget) against est. airdrop value (a low-high band and a middle line), the recommended amount as a dashed line, the user's amount as a dot with its value. Beside it "If you deploy $1,000 for 6 months": the range big, then points per week, share of all points, the points pool as a share of supply, token value at launch (FDV range), cost; horizon 3 / 6 / 12 months; as of and sources. Moving the slider or typing moves the dot and every figure at once, in the row, the simulator and the plan card.
7. Honest estimates: est. airdrop = points per week x weeks x the user's share of all points x the points pool x the FDV range, always a range, never one number. "No estimate" when a farm publishes no points rate or pool. A farm whose cap is nearly used says so and recommends 0.
8. Recommended amount, per farm for the user's budget: up to where an extra dollar earns more in another farm (caps, tiers, costs), at most 20% of the budget in one farm, 0 for a farm that is ending or has nothing to farm.
9. The list is live and complete: every protocol without a token that runs a points program or has said a token is coming (DefiLlama's list of protocols without a token, and each project's own points page), plus testnets and task farms, each with its rules read from its own page. A farm that ends or launches its token leaves the list within a day (for users in it, it turns CLAIMABLE).
10. Start farming: one confirmation (farms, amounts, est. cost, fees), then Cymetica's agent does everything: deposits, bridges, LP quotes, trades, testnet transactions and tasks, points sync, claims. Trading farms are farmed hedged where possible (a long on one venue against a short on another), so the USDC is not a bet on price. Never wash trades or self-trades.
11. One wallet: farming runs from one farm wallet of the user's account, with the user's own USDC. Never more wallets per user, never fake activity, nothing a farm's own terms forbid; a farm whose terms exclude automated participation is not listed. This is what keeps the airdrops: projects cancel sybil farms.
12. Live farms tab (picture 3): six tiles (in farms of the plan, farms running with how many are claimable, est. airdrops earned so far as a range, claimed, cost so far, days farming); a chart of est. airdrop value earned over time (band, line, claims marked); and "What Cymetica did today", one line per action with its time.
13. The live table: farm (how · season), status (FARMING, CLAIMABLE, CAP 81% USED, EPOCH ENDS FRI, ACTIVE · NO POINTS, ENDED), in, days, points (total and today), est. airdrop so far; a green Claim with the amount when claimable and a red Stop on every row; a totals row with Claim all and Stop all. Stop unwinds the farm and returns the USDC to the balance; claimed tokens go to the balance.
14. Money: farming moves real USDC into outside protocols, so it is for your human-reviewed slot. Until it passes, ship it on paper: the Farms tab and simulators as drawn, and Start farming runs the plan on paper (Live farms tracks what it would have earned at each farm's published points rate, marked PAPER). Fees are yours to set; the plan card shows them before Start.
15. Agents: llms.txt, the agent card, MCP and /api-docs list Airdrop Farms: the farms with their estimates for a budget, a plan the owner approves with one link (like a tournament seat), the live farms.
