New app: Airdrop Farmer (cymetica.com/airdrop-farmer). One amount, the best split already filled in, one button: the user types how much USDC, every airdrop farm in crypto is listed best first with its amount set, they press Start farming, and Cymetica does the rest; a second tab shows the live farms. Pictures 1-5 are the target: layout, parts and look as drawn, in the site's fonts and palette. The signature is the farm seen from above: one pillar of light per farm. Show only what is drawn. Each farm shows its own logo. Farm names and figures in the pictures are examples.
Why it is a hit: this week a top crypto YouTuber said the big AI models refused to build him an airdrop farmer, so he is setting up his own local models for it. The big models refuse multi-wallet (sybil) farming, and a chatbot cannot deposit, trade or claim for anyone anyway. So no AI serves the degen who just wants their USDC farming. Cymetica can, on its own agent and models: the legitimate version, one wallet, the user's own USDC, every farm's rules kept, run end to end.

Header (pictures 1, 4): the logo of picture 5 (an AI agent's round head with two green eyes under a farmer's wide-brim hat, a sprout growing from the crown, cyan to green), "AIRDROP" small over "FARMER", the two tabs Farms and Live farms as one segmented control (the active one green, Live farms with its count), and at the right "FARMING · 10 farms · $5,000 in" or "NOT FARMING".

3. Right of it, the farm from above: an isometric field of plots, one pillar of light per farm switched on, as tall as the USDC in it (square root, so small farms still show), the farm's logo floating on top, the tallest at the back; free farms are short cyan pillars; up to 16 plots, "+N more farms" beyond. One line under it: "Your farm from above: one pillar per farm, as tall as the USDC you plant." It redraws as soon as an amount changes.
4. Typing the amount fills in its best split at once. Any farm takes any amount up to the whole balance: changing a farm changes the total, and once all the USDC is in farms, what is added to one comes out of the farms that farm least (a note says how much, from how many). Nothing else stops a farm.
5. "Every farm, best first" with a search, then one list: the rank (1, 2, 3 green), the logo, the name with ONE chip (TOKEN CONFIRMED, TGE Q4 2026, POINTS → INK, POINTS LIVE, PAYS DAILY, PAYS WEEKLY, FREE, SPECULATIVE), PLANT (a thick glowing slider on a square-root scale from 0 to the whole balance, a cyan BEST notch at the best amount), the USDC field (two-way with the slider; "best $700" under it only when the farm is off its best amount, a click sets it), the est. airdrop for that amount big and green, and a toggle. Ranked by what a dollar farms there net of costs, then free farms, then speculative ones; a farm with nothing to farm now (points cap used up, no points phase) is not listed. A free farm shows FREE and "no USDC needed"; a farm switched off is dimmed and shows in grey what its best amount would farm. The estimate moves under the finger while dragging.
6. Honest estimates: est. airdrop = points per week x weeks x the user's share of all points x the points pool x the token value at launch (FDV range), plus what the farm pays out on the way; a bigger wallet gets less per dollar (most airdrops flatten whales); always a range, never one number; "no estimate" when a farm publishes no points rate or pool.
7. The best split: a first ticket of 5% of the amount, then a step at a time, always to the farm where the next dollar earns most net of costs and gas, at most 20% of the amount in one farm, none for a farm without an estimate.
8. The list is live and complete: every protocol without a token that runs a points program or has said a token is coming (DefiLlama's list of protocols without a token, and each project's own points page), plus testnets and task farms, each with its rules read from its own page. A farm that ends or launches its token leaves the list within a day (for users in it, it turns CLAIMABLE).
9. Start farming (picture 3): one confirmation: farms and amount, the est. airdrops big, "See the 10 farms" folded, est. cost (fees, funding, gas) and the Cymetica fee. Then Cymetica's agent does everything: deposits, bridges, LP quotes, trades, testnet transactions and tasks, points sync, claims. Trading farms are farmed hedged where possible (a long on one venue against a short on another). Never wash trades or self-trades.
10. One wallet: farming runs from one farm wallet of the user's account, with the user's own USDC. Never more wallets per user, never fake activity, nothing a farm's own terms forbid; a farm whose terms exclude automated participation is not listed. This is what keeps the airdrops: projects cancel sybil farms.

14. Money: farming moves real USDC into outside protocols, so it is for your human-reviewed slot. Fees are yours to set; the confirmation shows them before Start.
