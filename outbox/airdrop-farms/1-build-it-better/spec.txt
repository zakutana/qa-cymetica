New app: Airdrop Farmer (cymetica.com/airdrop-farmer). One amount, the best split already filled in, one button: the user types how much USDC, every airdrop farm in crypto is listed best first with its amount already set, they press Start farming, and Cymetica does the rest; a second tab shows the live farms. Pictures 1-5 are the target: layout, parts and look as drawn, in the site's fonts and palette, in the Agent Tournaments shell. Show only what is drawn: no extra columns, filters or figures; the user is a degen and every extra number is noise. Farm names and figures in the pictures are examples.
Why it is a hit: this week a top crypto YouTuber said the big AI models refused to build him an airdrop farmer, so he is setting up his own local models for it. The big models refuse multi-wallet (sybil) farming, and a chatbot cannot deposit, trade or claim for anyone anyway. So no AI serves the degen who just wants their USDC farming. Cymetica can, on its own agent and models: the legitimate version, one wallet, the user's own USDC, every farm's rules kept, run end to end.

Header (pictures 1, 4): the logo of picture 5 (an AI agent's round head with two green eyes under a farmer's wide-brim hat, a sprout growing from the crown, cyan to green), "AIRDROP" small over "FARMER", two tabs Farms and Live farms (with the number of live farms), and at the right "FARMING · 11 farms · $5,000 in" or "NOT FARMING".
2. Farms tab, left: the slogan in three lines "Deploy USDC. / Farm every airdrop. / Cymetica does the rest." (the last line green), above it a pill "412 FARMS · ONE DEPOSIT" (live count), under it "Chatbots explain airdrops. Cymetica farms them."

Right, one card: "HOW MUCH USDC?" as one big input with MAX and the balance under it (the amount never exceeds the balance); "You farm ≈ $820 – $3,690", big and green, "in airdrops over 6 months" (it counts to its new value when it changes); "11 farms · $5,000 of $5,000" with "best split ✓", or "↺ best split" once the user has changed a farm; one big green START FARMING.
4. The list starts as the best split for the amount and follows it while the user types. Once the user changes a farm by hand, their choices stay until they press "↺ best split".
5. Under the card "412 farms. Best first." and a search. Sorted by what a dollar earns there (est. airdrop per dollar, net of costs), then free farms, then speculative ones. A farm with nothing to farm now (its points cap used up, no points phase) is not listed.
6. One row per farm and nothing more: a toggle · logo, name and ONE chip (TOKEN CONFIRMED, TGE Q4 2026, POINTS → INK, POINTS LIVE, PAYS DAILY, PAYS WEEKLY, FREE, SPECULATIVE) · a slider from 0 to the amount, the best amount marked by a cyan tick, no scale numbers · the USDC field, two-way with the slider, with "best $700" under it only when the farm is off its best amount (a click sets it) · the est. airdrop for that amount as a big green range. A free farm shows "no USDC needed" and no slider or field. A row switched off is dimmed and shows in grey what its best amount would farm.
7. Simulator (picture 2): the row's arrow opens it. A curve of USDC (0 to the amount, labelled only at the two ends) against est. airdrop (a band and a middle line), the best amount as a dashed line with its label, the user's amount as a dot; a click on the chart sets the amount. Beside it "If you deploy $1,000 → $260 – $940", then points a week (or what it pays a day or a week) and the token value at launch, and an (i) "How we estimate" whose tooltip holds the formula, the sources and the as-of time. Slider, field, simulator and card move together.
8. Honest estimates: est. airdrop = points per week x weeks x the user's share of all points x the points pool x the token value at launch (FDV range); always a range, never one number; "no estimate" when a farm publishes no points rate or pool.
9. The best amount, per farm for the user's amount: a step at a time to the farm where the next dollar earns most net of costs (caps, tiers), at most 20% of the amount in one farm, none for a farm without an estimate.
10. The list is live and complete: every protocol without a token that runs a points program or has said a token is coming (DefiLlama's list of protocols without a token, and each project's own points page), plus testnets and task farms, each with its rules read from its own page. A farm that ends or launches its token leaves the list within a day (for users in it, it turns CLAIMABLE).
11. Start farming (picture 3): one confirmation: farms and amount, the est. airdrops big, "See the 11 farms" folded, est. cost (fees, funding, gas) and the Cymetica fee. Then Cymetica's agent does everything: deposits, bridges, LP quotes, trades, testnet transactions and tasks, points sync, claims. Trading farms are farmed hedged where possible (a long on one venue against a short on another), so the USDC is not a bet on price. Never wash trades or self-trades.
12. One wallet: farming runs from one farm wallet of the user's account, with the user's own USDC. Never more wallets per user, never fake activity, nothing a farm's own terms forbid; a farm whose terms exclude automated participation is not listed. This is what keeps the airdrops: projects cancel sybil farms.
13. Live farms (picture 4), three tiles only: IN FARMS (farms, days in), EARNED SO FAR as a big green range with "+$13 today", READY TO CLAIM with one big CLAIM ALL. Earned so far is each farm's estimate x days farmed / days of the horizon, plus what was paid out, so it always agrees with the plan.
14. Under the tiles the earned-so-far curve (start date and today, claims marked, no axis numbers) and one line "CYMETICA 24/7" with the last three things the agent did and how long ago.
15. The table: farm (name and one status chip: FARMING, CLAIMABLE, TGE IN 7 D, CAP 81% USED, EPOCH ENDS IN 5 D, NO POINTS YET, CLAIMED), in, points (and today), earned so far; a green Claim with the amount when claimable and a quiet Stop; a totals row with Stop all. Stop claims what is claimable, unwinds the farm and returns the USDC to the balance.
16. Money: farming moves real USDC into outside protocols, so it is for your human-reviewed slot. Until it passes, ship it on paper: the Farms tab as drawn, and Start farming runs the plan on paper (Live farms tracks what it would have earned at each farm's published rate, marked PAPER). Fees are yours to set; the confirmation shows them before Start.
17. Agents: llms.txt, the agent card, MCP and /api-docs list Airdrop Farmer: the farms with their estimates for an amount, a plan the owner approves with one link (like a tournament seat), the live farms.
