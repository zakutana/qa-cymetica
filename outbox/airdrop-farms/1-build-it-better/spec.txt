Rebuild of Airdrop Farmer (https://cymetica.com/airdrop-farmer). Everything is at https://github.com/zakutana/qa-cymetica/tree/a6bc873ff47a24dd857d769ce4da743722357d86/outbox/airdrop-farms: the working code (open mock.html), the full spec with every number (SPEC.md) and the target pictures in pictures/ (1-farms, 2-farm-list, 3-start-farming, 4-live-farms, 5-logo; "picture N" below means these). Build layout, parts and look as drawn, in the site's fonts and palette; show only what is drawn; names and figures in the pictures are examples. Live with real USDC from day one, no paper mode (item 14).
Why: the live build misses the idea. It farms nothing anyone will airdrop: 7 of its 8 programs are chains whose token is already live (ETH, ARB, OP, HYPE, BNB, AVAX, POL), the 8th is LI.FI routing. It only bridges and swaps stablecoins "on a jittered human cadence", the pattern airdrop programs filter out as sybil, and every farm wallet is created, funded and driven by Cymetica on the same routes, so they can read as one cluster. Its fee budget (/api/v1/airdrop-farm/config: 0.35% of the deployment a day on Balanced, 1% on Degen) lets fees take up to 63% of a deployment in 6 months on Balanced and all of it in 100 days on Degen, and deployments are open. The user cannot pick farms or amounts and sees no estimate.
The idea: one amount of USDC, split across the real points programs, testnets and task farms of protocols with no token yet, and a live view of what each farms. Keep: one farm wallet per person, never pooled; every step a real transaction; stop any time back to USDC; the 10% of realized profit fee.

4. Typing the amount fills in its best split at once. Any farm takes any amount up to the whole balance: changing a farm changes the total; once all the USDC is in farms, what is added to one comes out of the farms that farm least (a note says how much, from how many).

"Every farm, hottest first" with a search, then one list sorted by HEAT: a flame and a score 0-100 from your own sentiment engine (how much the groups talk about the farm now); the flame grows and lights with it, 85+ glowing. Then the logo, name with ONE chip (TOKEN CONFIRMED, TGE Q4 2026, POINTS → INK, POINTS LIVE, PAYS DAILY, PAYS WEEKLY, FREE, SPECULATIVE) and an EARLY tag (sprout) on a farm few talk about yet that the engine sees potential in; PLANT (a thick glowing slider on a square-root scale to the whole balance, a cyan BEST notch at the best amount); the USDC field (two-way with the slider; "best $700" under it when off the best amount); the est. airdrop big and green; a toggle. A free farm shows FREE; a farm switched off is dimmed and shows in grey what its best amount would farm. The estimate moves under the finger while dragging.
6. Honest estimates: est. airdrop = points per week x weeks x the user's share of all points x the points pool x the token value at launch (FDV range), plus what the farm pays out on the way; a bigger wallet gets less per dollar; always a range; "no estimate" when a farm publishes no points rate or pool.
7. The best split: a first ticket of 5% of the amount, then a step at a time to the farm where the next dollar earns most net of costs and gas, at most 20% in one farm, none without an estimate.
8. Every airdrop farm in crypto, live: every protocol without a token that runs a points program or has said a token is coming, on every chain, plus every testnet and task farm with rewards (DefiLlama's list of protocols without a token, each project's points page, the big airdrop trackers), rules read from its own page. Only farms that pass a quality gate, no lame or dubious ones: a token still to come and points running now; $5M+ TVL, or a public funded team for a testnet or task farm; published rules never changed against users; audited contracts, no unresolved exploit; no paid points, extra wallets, referral chains or social tasks; terms that allow automation. Re-checked daily; a farm that ends, launches its token or fails the gate leaves within a day (for users in it, CLAIMABLE or why).
9. Start farming (picture 3): one confirmation: farms and amount, est. airdrops big, "See the 10 farms" folded, est. cost (fees, funding, gas) and the fee. Then the agent does everything: deposits, bridges, LP quotes, trades, testnet transactions and tasks, points sync, claims. Trading farms hedged where possible. Never wash trades or self-trades.
10. One wallet, the user's own USDC: never more wallets per user, never activity for its own sake, nothing a farm's terms forbid. Projects cancel sybil farms; this keeps the airdrops.
