New app: Airdrop Farmer (cymetica.com/airdrop-farmer). One amount, the best split already filled in, one button: the user types how much USDC, every airdrop farm in crypto is listed best first with its amount set, they press Start farming, and Cymetica does the rest; a second tab shows the live farms. Pictures 1-5 are the target: layout, parts and look as drawn, in the site's fonts and palette and in the Agent Tournaments family (its honeycomb weave behind the top, its glowing cards, its table felt). Show only what is drawn. Each farm shows its own logo. Farm names and figures in the pictures are examples.
Why it is a hit: this week a top crypto YouTuber said the big AI models refused to build him an airdrop farmer, so he is setting up his own local models for it. The big models refuse multi-wallet (sybil) farming, and a chatbot cannot deposit, trade or claim for anyone anyway. So no AI serves the degen who just wants their USDC farming. Cymetica can, on its own agent and models: the legitimate version, one wallet, the user's own USDC, every farm's rules kept, run end to end.

Right, the YOUR FARM PLAN card with a glowing border: one big amount input with a USDC pill and MAX, the balance under it; the felt (the tournament table's cloth) holding "YOU FARM $900 – $3,890 in airdrops over 6 months", which counts to each new value; boxes FARMS, IN and CONFIRMED (farms whose token is confirmed); a big green START FARMING; top right "BEST SPLIT ✓", or "↺ BEST SPLIT" after a hand change.
4. Typing the amount fills in its best split at once. Any farm takes any amount up to the whole balance: changing a farm changes the total, and once all the USDC is in farms, what is added to one comes out of the farms that farm least (a note says how much, from how many). Nothing else stops a farm.
5. Under the hero "Farms 412", one line "Best first. Drag a ring or type an amount: the airdrop updates as you go." and a search. Cards, three to a row: estimated farms by what a dollar earns there net of costs, then free farms, then speculative ones. A farm with nothing to farm now (points cap used up, no points phase) is not listed.
6. Each card (picture 2): the farm's logo, name, ONE chip (TOKEN CONFIRMED, TGE Q4 2026, POINTS → INK, POINTS LIVE, PAYS DAILY, PAYS WEEKLY, FREE, SPECULATIVE) and a toggle. The dial: a 270° ring with faint ticks round a small felt; the amount as a glowing arc with a knob to drag (square-root scale from 0 to the whole balance, so small and big bets both read), the best amount as a small cyan marker; in the middle EST. AIRDROP, the range big and green, "in 6 months". Under it three boxes: YOU DEPLOY (typed), BEST (✓ when set, else a click sets it), PTS / WEEK (or PAYS / WEEK, PAYS / DAY). A free farm: dotted full ring, FREE, no USDC needed. A farm switched off is dimmed and shows in grey what its best amount would farm. Ring, field and plan card move together; hovering the ring shows how the estimate is made.
7. Honest estimates: est. airdrop = points per week x weeks x the user's share of all points x the points pool x the token value at launch (FDV range), plus what the farm pays out on the way; a bigger wallet gets less per dollar (most airdrops flatten whales); always a range, never one number; "no estimate" when a farm publishes no points rate or pool.
8. The best split: a first ticket of 5% of the amount, then a step at a time, always to the farm where the next dollar earns most net of costs and gas, at most 20% of the amount in one farm, none for a farm without an estimate.
9. The list is live and complete: every protocol without a token that runs a points program or has said a token is coming (DefiLlama's list of protocols without a token, and each project's own points page), plus testnets and task farms, each with its rules read from its own page. A farm that ends or launches its token leaves the list within a day (for users in it, it turns CLAIMABLE).
10. Start farming (picture 3): one confirmation: farms and amount, the felt with the est. airdrops big, "See the 10 farms" folded, est. cost (fees, funding, gas) and the Cymetica fee. Then Cymetica's agent does everything: deposits, bridges, LP quotes, trades, testnet transactions and tasks, points sync, claims. Trading farms are farmed hedged where possible (a long on one venue against a short on another). Never wash trades or self-trades.
11. One wallet: farming runs from one farm wallet of the user's account, with the user's own USDC. Never more wallets per user, never fake activity, nothing a farm's own terms forbid; a farm whose terms exclude automated participation is not listed. This is what keeps the airdrops: projects cancel sybil farms.

Fees are yours to set; the confirmation shows them before Start.
