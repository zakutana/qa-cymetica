# Segment C: DEPOSIT farms on Ethereum and EVM L2s (as of 2026-10-24)

Method. Starting population = DefiLlama `/protocols` (fetched 2026-10-24), symbol "-" (no token tracked), TVL >= $5M, categories lending / yield / basis / stable / LST / restaking / RWA / liquidity manager / DEX / vault allocators: 272 rows (all saved in the scratchpad as `llama-protocols.json`). I checked 100+ of them (about 45 individually, the rest as groups) plus the airdrops.io, AirdropAlert and DropsTab trackers (sitemaps and pages), project sites and docs, CoinGecko pages (token trading or not), and the DefiLlama hacks feed (exploit history).

Headline: **16 farms meet the definition (11 verified recent + 5 partial); 12 are missing from Cymetica's list; 5 more tier-C candidates need a human check (not counted).** Three flags: (1) Axis's TOKEN CONFIRMED chip is a news-matcher name collision (see section 3); (2) Saturn is EEA/US geo-blocked; (3) the shared web-search budget ran out mid-run, so the second half is fetch-only.

Limits you should know about.
* The shared web-search budget (200 per turn, all agents) ran out partway through. The second half of the checks used only direct page fetches, not search.
* X/Twitter, cryptorank, DefiLlama /airdrops and DappRadar were not readable (403/402). Where a claim rests on a tracker page I say so and give its "last updated" date.
* Tracker pages often have a bulk "updated" stamp (many say 2026-07-18 or 2026-09-30) that does not prove the program was checked that day.
* TVL is DefiLlama `tvl` (excludes borrowed) on 2026-10-24. "Audits" = DefiLlama audit links. "Exploit history" = DefiLlama hacks feed (1,318 incidents).

Confidence: **H** = two independent sources or the project's own live page; **M** = one recent tracker plus a partial first-party signal; **L** = thin, stale or conflicting; needs a human check before any deposit.
