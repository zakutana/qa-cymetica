## 4. DISCREPANCIES WITH CYMETICA'S LIST (for filing)

1. **Fables - POINTS LIVE is stale.** FABLES token is live on Robinhood Chain, points stopped 2026-10-19, claim open until 2026-11-20. It now fails "no token live".
2. **Robinhood Chain network - chip TOKEN CONFIRMED has no source.** Cryptoast (2026-09-01), airdropalert and airdrops.io all say Robinhood has confirmed no token, no points, no snapshot. The only "points to a token" on the chain is Lighter (LIT already trades) and Arcus (own token confirmed, not the chain's). Ask what Cymetica saw.
3. **Tempo network - chip POINTS LIVE has no source.** tempo.xyz (fetched 2026-10-24) names no points, rewards or token; airdrops.io (2026-03-23) says no points programme; pre-launch report said no token. Its only DefiLlama-listed apps are the native stablecoin DEX and a Morpho curator.
4. **Zircuit Staking** - ZRC claim is open (Season 1 = 7% of supply) but non-transferable until Mainnet Phase 2; a gate edge case, check whether the token trades.
5. Four of the 49 entries are chains although the gate says "not a chain"; observed, not judged.
6. Extended is the biggest app on Arc but its weekly points ended (docs 2026-10-06). If it is added, label it "closing, final competition to ~2026-11-03", not "points live".
7. Arc's POINTS LIVE rests on Arc House "Architects" contribution points (email/LinkedIn sign-up, approved content/events/projects), not on-chain or deposit points. Against the gate ("deposit farm", "usable from your one farm wallet") that is a soft mismatch; observed, not judged.
