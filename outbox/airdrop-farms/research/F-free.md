# F-free: FREE farms (testnets, nodes, quests): verification of Cymetica's 22 + missing farms

Date of research: 2026-10-24. Segment F of 7.

## Method and limits (read first)

* **WebSearch ran out mid-task** (shared 200-call pool; other researchers used it). About 12 searches were made before it stopped. Everything after that came from:
  1. **Galxe's own GraphQL API** (`graphigo.prd.galaxy.eco/query`, queried live 2026-10-24): campaign status, start/end dates, participant counts, `tgeInfo.status`, `token`, `backers`, space links. This is primary data for what Cymetica's "Galxe quest" farms actually are.
  2. **CoinGecko / CoinPaprika APIs** for "does a token exist".
  3. **Project sites and airdrops.io detail pages fetched directly** (airdrops.io pages carry an "updated" date; most are Sept-Oct 2026). airdrops.io is an aggregator, not the project: its token/airdrop statements are second-hand. Funding on those pages comes from the page text; where I could confirm it on the project's own site I say so.
* Not readable: DefiLlama, CryptoRank, Dropstab, ICO Drops (403/404/paid API), Layer3 and Zealy (JS pages). **Layer3 and Zealy were not checked.** Galxe's Alpha `project{fundraise,investors}` endpoint returned "no healthy upstream" on every attempt, so funding amounts for Table 1 rows without a named raise are "not disclosed / not found", not "none".
* Table 2 is anchored on airdrops.io (Oct 2026) plus Galxe, not on a 2026 web-search sweep. Treat it as a strong list, not a complete one.

## Key finding about HOW Cymetica's free list is built

Their 22 "free" rows look like **Galxe spaces whose space-level flag `tgeInfo.status` is `TGEUpcoming`/`Presale` plus any campaign with `status = Active`**. Both are weak signals:

* **1,278 Galxe spaces** carry TGEUpcoming/Presale (805 + 472, queried today). The flag is self-set by the space admin and is almost never cleared after a TGE (Galxe itself says `TokenReleased` for INFINIT, Joltify and Zoom Manor, but Rivalz, Sonic SVM, Dill, Travala are still "UnSpecified"/"Presale" with live tokens).
* **`status = Active` does not mean running.** Many "Active" campaigns are `Parent` campaigns or points campaigns with **no start and no end date**, left over from 2023-2025 (Dill roadmap, Rivalz Intel Testnet, Spindle S1/S2, Avitus 2024 testnet, Fewcha 2023 NFT, Joltify 2024). Of 22 rows only 9 have a campaign **started in 2026** (Checkpoint, INFINIT, Overlayer, Lighthouse, 4Dlabs, ADREVA, Coincamps, Travala, Sonic SVM).
* The top of Galxe's trending list of active campaigns (9,151 active in total) is **USDT giveaways**, not airdrop points: the first five are Travala, Coincamps, dkarbon, IndiGG and Asterium, all paying USDT. Of the 1,500 trending campaigns I downloaded, only a handful of spaces had real investors and a 2026 start (Lighthouse, 4Dlabs, DataHive AI, Ducat, Sigma Browser).
* Suggested filter for the app: require (a) `Galxe tgeInfo.status != TokenReleased`, (b) token lookup on CoinGecko/CoinPaprika by project name returns nothing, (c) at least one campaign with `startTime` in the last 90 days or `endTime` in the future, (d) reward type is points/XP/role, not USDT.
