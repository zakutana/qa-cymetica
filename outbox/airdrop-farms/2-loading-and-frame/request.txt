Airdrop Farmer (https://cymetica.com/airdrop-farmer): two changes, a loading screen and no frame round the app. The working loading screen, all of its code in one file: https://github.com/zakutana/qa-cymetica/blob/4d4eb80a790dc438d9627c8f4516ad7881d1014c/outbox/airdrop-farms/loading/index.html (HTML, CSS and JS; save it and open it in a browser to watch it run, add ?cold=1 for the 9 s cold start). The 16 logos it uses: https://github.com/zakutana/qa-cymetica/tree/4d4eb80a790dc438d9627c8f4516ad7881d1014c/outbox/airdrop-farms/loading/icons . Pictures 1-3 are it frozen at 12, 55 and 100 %, picture 4 is the frame. Hook-up: call AFBoot.done('page' | 'config' | 'farms' | 'plan' | 'status') as each request returns. Keep the app's name, logo, fonts and palette; this changes nothing else.
Why: the page takes about 2 s and GET /api/v1/airdrop-farm/farms 1.5 s warm and up to 9 s cold; signed in, /plan reads the wallets on 9 networks too. Meanwhile the user sees a nearly empty page with the line "Reading the farms..." and the footer text. And a band of another colour runs round the whole app, on both sides and along the top.
1. Loading screen, over the whole page until the page has drawn its first frame (pictures 1-3, like the Universe's on /company). The farm from above wakes up: a 4 x 4 isometric field of dark plots; as each is read, the Farmer logo (drifting from side to side over the field, bobbing) drops a seed of light into it and a pillar of light grows with a round farm logo on top. Sixteen logos ship with the page (bundled, so they show before /farms answers).
2. Under it, a big percentage in Archivo Black (a small cyan %), one line in JetBrains Mono, letter-spaced, saying what is being read ("OPENING YOUR FARM", "READING 43 FARMS" with the count in green, "READING YOUR WALLETS", "CHECKING YOUR FARM WALLET", "READY"), and a thin cyan-to-green bar with a light running along it.
3. The percentage is the real work, not a timer: the requests the page waits for each carry a share (the page 12, /config 8, /farms 48, /plan 22, /status 10, the last two only signed in). The number creeps inside the share of the request still running (never past 85 % of it) so it never freezes, and reaches 100 only when everything is in. Then the screen fades out in 0.7 s.
4. Only transform and opacity animate, so it keeps moving while the page builds. Site colours only (#0a0e17 ground, cyan #00cfff, green #00ff88). prefers-reduced-motion slows it down. No spinner, no skeleton.
5. The frame (picture 4): #adf.afm has its own solid background (#070B10), and it sits inside #main-content, which pads 32 px at both sides, over the site's page gradient. So the app is a flat panel of a different colour inset into the page, with a visible band on the left, the right and along the top (under the Back bar). Remove it: give .afm no background of its own (transparent) and no side padding, so the site's gradient runs behind the app edge to edge; drop any border or radius that draws the panel.
6. Check at 1440 and 1920 px: nothing but the site's own background behind the header, the plan panel and the field, no line or colour step at the edges, and the Back link still in place. Also with the Start dialog open (its dark overlay must cover the whole screen, not a panel).
