## How "hot" was measured, and what it is worth

* **airdrops.io /hot** (community votes, fetched 2026-10-29): pages 1-3 = 18 + 18 + 3 = 39 entries. The ° number is unlabeled on the site; I use the /hot value (homepage "Hottest" shows slightly different values, a few points apart). **The ° on an individual project page is not usable** (it reads "-4", "0", "1" for most pages: not loaded), so a project that is not on /hot has no vote number here.
* **Cross-tracker frequency:** alphadrops.net "Top 50 Free Airdrops" (updated 2026-10-29, its own order), airdrops.io "Best crypto airdrops to farm right now" (2026-09-21, checked 2026-10-12), Bitrue "Best airdrops October" (2026-10-13), DropsTab /activities (2026-10-29), DappRadar/CoinLaunch via search.
* **Size:** DefiLlama API pulled 2026-10-29 (TVL for tokenless entries, OI from `overview/open-interest`). DefiLlama hacks feed for exploits.
* **Not readable:** CryptoRank /drophunting (HTTP 403), DefiLlama /airdrops (402/403 per earlier research), coinlaunch.space (403). Not used.
* **Rank rule:** tier 1 (rows 1-7) = on /hot top 15 AND in at least two other sources, ordered by /hot score. Tier 2 (rows 8-15) = ordered by number of independent mentions in current (October 2026) sources, then 7-day momentum/news, then size. Tie-breaks stated: TxFlow (alphadrops #3 + CoinLaunch guide) ranks above Pacifica (alphadrops #32 + CoinLaunch; its third mention is an August guide, not current) on +18% OI in 7 days and a season that started 2026-10-12; Hibachi ranks above DuelBet because DuelBet is single-source and a casino; Decibel (airdrops.io page 2026-10-16, TGE Q4) and Upshift (TVL $454M, alphadrops #19) fill the last two places because they are the only remaining farms on alphadrops or airdrops.io with the token still unconfirmed or Q4, and both are in Airdrop Farmer. **Saturn passes every filter (geo-block only) and is ranked out of the 15 for one reason: it is unavailable in the EEA, so a Czech wallet cannot use it.** Anyone outside the EEA/US can move it into the table: TGE Q4, Season 2 to 2026-12-08, TVL $153.6M. I did not reorder by taste. Variational has the biggest size (OI $977M) and two outside articles, but a lower /hot score than Arcus, so it is 5th by the rule.
* **Reading the hot list:** of 39 /hot entries only 7 are clean paid farms (rows 1-7); DuelBet (casino) passes on page text and is flagged; TBook, Startale and Monolith pass but are small. The rest are free social/task farms, casinos, or already-live tokens (see section 3).
