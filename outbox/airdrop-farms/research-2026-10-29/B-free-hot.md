## Method and limits (read first)

* **Trackers actually read:** airdrops.io Hot p1-2, Testnet p1-3, Confirmed p1-2, Latest p1-2, Speculative p1-2, Calendar, plus ~27 airdrops.io detail pages (each has an "updated" date); AirdropAlert testnet page; guide sites found by search (usethebitcoin, Bitrue, coingabbar, bittime, Cointelegraph/Decrypt/Bankless/Fortune news). **Not readable:** CryptoRank (403; only seen in search-result titles), DropsTab (404), Galxe GraphQL (now 404, was working on 10-24), Layer3/Zealy (JS). CoinGecko search rate-limited (429) after 4 answers. So "trackers" below is a **partial count** of what I could read, not a full one.
* **Aggregators lag the project.** Two cases found today: airdrops.io (updated 26 Oct) still says Ritual has "no mainnet date" while Ritual launched its token on 26 Oct; airdrops.io (17 Jun) says Ekiden has an Aptos testnet while Ekiden's own docs now say mainnet only. Where a row rests on an airdrops.io page older than ~4 months I say so.
* **Ranking rule (mine):** airdrop confirmed by the team > size and named investors of the raise > number of trackers listing it > freshness (page updated in the last 6 weeks, campaign visibly running). "Agent can do it alone" is reported, not scored. Projects with no raise found are kept but ranked below funded ones and flagged.
* Hard filters applied: token not live; campaign running this week; no deposit; no KYC mentioned anywhere (no page mentions KYC for any row below; Sweep Finance, Youmio, Theoriq, Ink, Variational do and were excluded).
