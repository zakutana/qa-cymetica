# For the spec, beyond what the mockup shows

What the live /company cannot do today and the spec has to ask for. One line each; add as they turn up.

- **Departments cannot hand work to each other.** A task's origin is only owner, manager or system (company answers of 2026-10-13), so a process like "a bug found anywhere goes to QA" or "a new build goes to QA for testing" runs only if the Manager routes it. The spec asks for owner-set processes: *when* an event happens, *which* department does *what*, chained, each with an optional human review before it starts or on its result, by the owner or a named person (Processes tab in the mockup). Noted 2026-10-16.
- **Testing becomes "Cymetica QA", the bug bounty as a department.** Its charter files each verified bug straight into the bug tracker under the owner's hunter name and wallet (no approval, the way an outside QA agent already works), reopens a known bug instead of filing it twice, and re-tests a claimed fix on the same ticket. The ET10 Cymetica pays for accepted bugs is shown lit on the department: in the office, in the rail, in its detail. Noted 2026-10-17.
- **Every department has a computer that opens its page on cymetica.com.** Cymetica QA: `/leaderboard/bugs` with My Bugs selected; Development and Design: `/leaderboard/features`; the lab: `/my-agents`; a trading desk: `/account`. Today no URL selects My Bugs (the tab is `data-filter="mine"`, read from nothing in the address), so the spec asks for `/leaderboard/bugs?filter=mine`. Noted 2026-10-17.
- **The Trading desk finds profitable agents and proves them in Agent Tournaments.** It scans agents by 30-day paper ROI, builds variants in the Bot Assembly Terminal (`/terminal`, its computer's link), and asks the owner for each seat (the platform already makes the owner approve every seat). A table's close is its verdict: top three keeps the agent for the next 7-day table, and the table's ET10 prize goes to the owner. Noted 2026-10-17.
- **The Trading desk's computer opens Experiments** (`experiments.html`): one card per experiment with its name, one big return (its bots' P&L since they joined, over what they staked), a line from start to now, the bots as green/red squares and the instructions for the agent running it; opened, the bots ranked by return with P&L and trades, Run / Pause, add and remove bots, rename, edit the instructions; "+ New experiment" is a name, the instructions and the bots, which start trading on paper. Seeded from our real clones (Wave 2, Grid, Head to head, Cap probe, Tournament); 500 USDC a bot is the grid's stake. Noted 2026-10-17.
- **A process is one sentence.** The owner writes "When a bug is found anywhere, Cymetica QA verifies it and files it" (or the Czech of it, or "…and I want to see the result"); the Manager reads the trigger, the department, the work and any review out of it, says what is missing when it cannot, and never adds a second process that says the same. No form fields. Noted 2026-10-17.
- **Only the Manager chats.** A department's detail holds its charter, not a conversation. Noted 2026-10-17.
- **The Universe: every company on an orbit round Cymetica.** A tab beside Investable, drawn to the operator's picture of an orrery: Cymetica in the middle (a collider of light), every company a space station on one of seven orbits that turn slowly, the biggest inside, in 3D (zoom out to all of it, in to one station, which the camera then rides along with); one deck per department, its name on a banner; a deck lights while its department works, and work flies to Cymetica as an arc of light. Live today `GET /api/v1/companies` lists only Investable companies (0 on 2026-10-17) and a private one answers 404, so it is in no galaxy at all; the spec asks for all of them, a private one locked. The mockup's 35 are samples (23 written, 12 generated). Noted 2026-10-18; space and orbits 2026-10-18.
- **Public or private is its own switch, apart from Investable.** Public: anyone opens it from the Universe and sees everything inside read only (office, departments, charters, processes, tasks, memory, settings); nothing can be changed. Private: its name, station and mission (the description) only. Today's public profile (`GET /api/v1/companies/{id}`) carries name, mission, status, departments with experts, scoreboard and entity, and only while Investable. Settings holds the switch; private by default, as Investable is. Noted 2026-10-18.
- **A company picks its station when it is created.** "Create your company" asks a name, a mission, a station (Spire, Biodome, Fortress, Ring station, Laser station, Vault, Data stack) and its colour (the site's four: green, cyan, blue, red), and public or private; Settings changes them later. Noted 2026-10-18.
- **The office and the Universe in 3D.** three.js with its WebGPU renderer (WebGL2 where a browser has none): the same rooms and corridors as the flat office, each room lit in the colour of what it does; the flat office stays one click away, and takes over where 3D cannot start (so does a flat picture of the Universe). Noted 2026-10-18.
- **The look to build to is the operator's two pictures, drawn live.** `look-office.webp` (the office on its asteroid) and `look-universe.webp` (the orrery) are renders the operator made; the mockup draws the same scenes live in WebGPU, matched to them piece by piece -- camera, wall heights, neon (the back walls lit along their top, the front walls along their foot), floors, furniture, the sky -- in the pictures' own colours: this is a game scene, outside the site's palette rule, orange "needs you" included (the operator, 2026-10-18). No painted picture and no downloaded texture is shown in their place. Its centre, Cymetica, is a collider of light: filaments round a white heart, beams, a ring of machinery, in white, cyan and blue. Noted 2026-10-18.
- **Every company has a Vault, always private.** One fixed room in the office holds what the company has earned, ET10 and USDC as two piles that grow; a visitor to a public company sees its door shut. Opened, it says how much each department and each app brought in. It replaces the "+ET10 earned" chip in the header. Noted 2026-10-18.
- **Portfolio: the apps a company built, and what they earn.** A tab listing every Build it Better app the account made (through the company or not), with Cymetica's own traffic numbers: visitors, uses, views, likes over 30 days (the mockup's are real, `GET /api/v1/build-it-better/apps/leaderboard`, read 2026-10-18). Two rewards it shows are proposals, not live: ET10 for each new unique user an app brings to the platform (asked in MSG-0192; `/traffic/mine` already counts them) and a USDC revenue share per use; the mockup's rates are placeholders. "Start a Company, build products, bring in users, earn real revenue." Noted 2026-10-18.
- **Each department shows what it spends and has a brake.** Live today (GET /api/v1/company/mine, 2026-10-17) meters units, priced 1:1 in dollars, and per department gives only the 7-day total (`units_7d`, `cost_usd_7d`); the only caps are company-wide (40 turns a day, 1 unit per 5 hours, 6 a week, extra usage up to 25 USDC a month). No tokens are shown anywhere. The spec asks per department for today's spend and the cost of a turn, a daily cap in dollars the owner sets (work over it waits for midnight), and for a department on a clock, runs a day. The session bar fills in one colour and turns red only near the limit. Noted 2026-10-17.
