New app, working name Pitch Cards, with one Profit Board for all of Cymetica. It sits in your normal page, under your header and above your chat bar. We supply the look and a few examples; the rest is yours to build your own way. Everything is at https://github.com/zakutana/qa-cymetica/tree/ffdc39e854da4f7e94ec43a844eb855bcdeead95/outbox/pitch-cards: the working code (run python serve.py, open http://127.0.0.1:8802/pitch-cards), and in 1-build-it-better/ the pictures (1-entry, 2-swipe, 3-amount, 4-profit-board, 5-tags, 6-phone; "picture N" below) and motion.webm, the animation to match.
The idea: pitches come to every user on the web. Nexus plays the investor walking into a TV show like Dragons' Den: it brings an idea and wants our money. The user swipes left to pass or right to put money in, then follows how the deal does.
Why the Profit Board: today what makes money on Cymetica is shown in scattered places, each with its own format and measure: /events/universe/movers, /fund-optimizations, /leaderboard#cards_72h, /fund-performance, /themes/performance and more. Everyone is waiting for the day Cymetica is profitable, and users ask on Telegram whether anyone is making money here yet, but no single page answers that. The Profit Board is that page: whoever wonders if money can be made on Cymetica yet comes and looks. It shows absolutely every profitable thing from all of Cymetica, always up to date, and nothing at a loss.
MUST STAY AS DRAWN: the look of the entry screen and the swipe, with all the animation. Please build these from our pc.css, pc.js and images, not a redraw of your own.
1. Entry screen (picture 1): your header, Back and chat bar stay. Below them the app bar of /airdrop-farmer: logo, PITCH over CARDS, tabs as one segmented control, a hairline under it, in its 1240 px column. Then the headline "Nexus pitches. / You swipe. / Every result public." in Archivo Black with the last line green, on the column's left edge; the live card under Nexus's fingertip, PASS and INVEST under the card; Nexus in front, standing out past the column's right edge; the room behind, fading into your page background on every side.
2. Sizes do not grow with the window: headline 58 px, card 400 x 530, Nexus at 72% of the render. His finger stays on the card at every desktop size. layout() in pc.js does this.
3. Use scene.webp (5120 x 2880) and nexus.webp (1840 x 2320) at that resolution. Nexus is a separate cut-out in front of the card.
4. A new pitch arrives: a line of light wipes the card on from his fingertip, the big number runs up, the green line draws while the faint peer lines fade in behind it, the sentence types out word by word, his eyes flare. His eyes pulse the whole time.
5. A card holds five things: a label line, the result as the big number, what it is, the chart, one or two sentences in Nexus's voice.
6. Swipe (picture 2): the card follows the finger and tilts, INVEST or PASS fills it in its colour, past 110 px it goes. The buttons do the same.
7. Left: the card flies off and the next pitch arrives.
8. Right (picture 3): the card turns over to the same face with the big number now the amount, the chart still in view, chips $10 $25 $50 All, buttons Not now and Invest $25, under the same thumb.
9. Invest: the card slides right and behind Nexus, his eyes flare, the next pitch arrives.
10. Phone (picture 6): your header and chat bar stay, his face looks over the card, the same motion.
11. Profit Board look (pictures 4, 5): built from the table and tabs of your own /themes/performance, under "What would have made you money on Cymetica." Every kind of investment has its own coloured tag, in your palette. Columns: Up since start, Peak, and one Days column with two numbers, how many days old it is / the day of its life the peak came (47 / 45), over a line of its whole life with the peak marked on it. Numbers green; nothing red, because nothing at a loss is on it.
12. Your page background on every tab; only Pitches adds the room.
