Redesign of the Quant Lab (https://cymetica.com/company/app/experiments), the page a company's owner opens to see what the lab searched, tested blind and started. Everything is at https://github.com/zakutana/qa-cymetica/tree/main/outbox/quant-lab-v2: the working mock (python serve.py, then http://127.0.0.1:8801/), README.md with every number's source, and the target pictures in 1-build-it-better/ ("picture N" below). Build layout, parts and look as drawn, in the site's fonts and palette (Archivo Black, JetBrains Mono; #0a0e17, #00ff88, #00cfff, #ff4444); show only what is drawn. The numbers in the pictures are our company 14's real ones (4 searches, 1,728 tried, 5 blind-tested, 0 passed) and, in pictures 2 and 4, a simulation of ET-33142 (the search unit is a whole bot setup; the winner starts as a paper bot with exactly that setup). Desktop only; no phone layout is asked for.
Why: the page today is paragraphs. A degen reads numbers and watches a picture: what the lab did, what it found and what it could not do should be clear in one look, with every explanation one hover away.
1. Header (picture 6): the mark, an Erlenmeyer flask that is a radar scope (a faint grid inside, a sweep going round, one green blip it finds, a ring going out each time the sweep hits it; the rim cyan to green), "QUANT LAB" (LAB green with a cyan-to-green underline), the line "Finds bots that win on data they never saw". Right of the crumb a pill with two views, Real (what the lab did) and Coming soon (how ET-33142 works, marked SIMULATED on the reactor); drop the pill once ET-33142 ships and the real data has that shape.
2. Six tiles with an icon each, from the search API: searches, failed (red when above 0; its tooltip names the failed searches and the reason as a plain label, never the raw error text), tried (combinations_tried), blind-tested, beat the bar, agents found (green when above 0).
3. The reactor (pictures 1, 2, 5): one wide dark screen under a rail of five stages, BUILD, BLAST, BLIND, THE BAR, FOUND; each stage is one word, one icon and big numbers, an animation of what the lab did. A stage button jumps to it; pause (or space) holds; paused, a stage click or left/right shows that stage's finished picture; the active button fills with the stage's progress.
4. BUILD: the setups assembled. Today a rule is a setup (16 rules, "no skills, no combos"); under ET-33142 a model or a Named Combination plus skills snap into one setup.
5. BLAST (picture 1): every setup times every market times every setting, a grid of cells lighting up under the coins' logos (CoinGecko, the source your /perpetual pages use), a counter running to the total tried, "on past prices".
6. BLIND: the search period and the held-back part behind a wall; the top candidates stepped through it and judged on the part they never saw.
7. THE BAR (picture 5): the threshold from your own method (z shown), the candidates flying at it; one that fails shatters red, one that passes goes through green.
8. FOUND (picture 2): the winning setup crushed into a core, a flash, the agent built up as a hologram under a scan beam, PROFITABLE AGENT FOUND, then its card trading the same setup on paper. With no winner the agent stays dark: NO PROFITABLE AGENT. The page never says "hero agent".
9. The badge beside the stages says what the reactor shows: REPLAY and the date of the last finished search; LAST N FAILED, then REPLAY and the date, in red, when the newest searches failed (a failure is a state of the lab, not a stage, so it has no screen); LIVE while a search runs. Live, the stages follow the WebSocket's search fields (done_jobs, total_jobs, percent, eta_seconds): the cells fill as the jobs do, with the count, the percent and the time left.
10. SEARCH NOW: your button (POST /quant-lab/search), green at the rail's right, your cost line as its tooltip; while a search runs it reads RUNNING N% and is disabled.
11. BLIND TEST (pictures 3, 4): the search's results as rows, one per candidate: rank, the setup as chips (model or combination green, skills blue), the coin with its logo, the settings (SL, TP, entry), search % to blind %, a gauge from the blind result to the bar (red short of it, green past it, the bar's value above it), a tick or a cross. The header: search id, date, tried, z.
12. BOTS: the owner's rule as chips (trades, profit, drawdown, windows, then the desk), Rule and Add bot as buttons; a card per paper bot: the agent's picture, name and coin, PROFITABLE / PAPER / RUNNING, SAME SETUP AS THE WINNER with its chips, the equity line, pause / vary / remove, trades, DD, SL and TP. With no bots one quiet line: "No profitable agent yet. Winners land here as paper bots."
13. Foot: What was tested with the memory count (the full list behind it), Log (the file), Can tune / can't (your can_tune and cannot_tune lists as short labels, the full sentences as tooltips), PAPER ONLY at the right.
14. Every explanation on the page is a hover; no paragraph anywhere. English only, the site's colours only.
