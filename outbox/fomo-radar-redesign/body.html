Is the 3× chance real?

Every signal this radar emits is written down before its outcome is known, then labelled 72 hours later from the prices that actually printed. The percentage next to a score is the share of past signals at that score that went 3× within 72 hours — not a model's opinion of the score.

1Persist

Each emitted signal is stored with its chain, token, timestamp, conviction, signal type and feature block — one row per token per 6-hour window, written before anyone knows what happens next.

2Observe

Robinhood Chain is indexed by no public price feed, so its price history is recorded from the chain's own trade prints (ETH per token on every curve trade), every 60 seconds for the first hour and every 15 minutes after that. Solana falls back to public pool candles.

3Label

72 hours later each signal gets: max multiple, whether it hit 3×, whether it rugged inside the first 5 minutes (price or liquidity down 90%), whether it rugged within 24 hours, and — on bonding curves — whether it survived graduation.

4Fit

An isotonic regression (monotone, so a higher score can never map to a lower probability) is refitted every 15 minutes over the rolling window of labelled signals. The Brier score shown is out of sample: fitted on the oldest 70% of the window, scored on the newest 30% it never saw, next to what the flat base rate scored on the same tail.

5Gate

A market-wide regime measure (median 24-hour return and the share of tracked tokens below their 24-hour open) scales the exposure recommendation. When the whole meme market is bleeding it goes to zero and the signals are suppressed — the answer to a bad tape is no exposure, not a better model.

Misses & gaps

A signal whose 72-hour window we could not price is marked unmeasurable and excluded from the fit — never counted as a miss. Below the minimum labelled sample the page shows insufficient history (n/N) and no percentage at all. Machine-readable: GET /api/v1/clones/fomo-radar/calibration?chain=robinhood|solana.

A $100 trade on every signal exit-policy backtest

—

The probability above asks whether a token touched 3×. Nobody sells at the top. This backtest replays each past signal against the complete price record it was labelled from, buys and sells a $100 position against the pool depth recorded at each moment, and applies a named exit rule. It is scored walk-forward with a gap longer than the longest horizon between what the model learned from and what it is tested on. It measures the past. It is not a forecast and not a guarantee.

Exit rules: hold to the horizon; a trailing stop 30% below the peak; or sell half at 2× and trail the rest by 30%. Horizons: 6, 24 and 72 hours. A result counts as positive when the position returned more than it cost after price impact. The headline test is the Matthews correlation (MCC) of the out-of-sample calls, which must reach 0.30; a negative expected value at every horizon is a valid result and is published as such. A result is withheld while the stored price records cover too few of the signals the radar emitted, because a figure on the tokens that kept trading would hide the ones that died. Machine-readable: GET /api/v1/clones/fomo-radar/backtest?chain=robinhood|solana.

Sibling wallets count once

entity resolution

A wallet costs nothing to create, so counting addresses counts what a farm can mint. Every qualified buyer on Robinhood Chain is resolved into an entity by union-find over four kinds of evidence, and conviction is summed over entities: wallets that always trade together cast one vote between them.

—
TXSame atomic transaction

Two wallets bought the same curve inside one transaction. One transaction has one sender paying for it — independent strangers cannot share one. The same block is a weaker version of the same link and is drawn separately.

$Common non-exchange funder

The address that submitted and paid for a wallet's first observed buy. A wallet that submits its own transaction draws no link, exchange and infrastructure addresses are excluded by a maintained list, and a funder with dozens of children is treated as infrastructure rather than one operator.

IDShared bundle id

Where the chain publishes a bundle id, wallets sharing one are the same submission. Robinhood Chain does not publish one today, so this rule reports itself unavailable instead of guessing a link.

∠Portfolio cosine similarity

Token portfolios are turned into vectors weighted by how rare each token is, and two wallets pointing the same way — sharing at least two tokens — are one entity. Sharing the day's hot coin is worth nothing here; sharing two coins nobody else touched is worth everything.

1How an entity votesonce

Once. Its score is its best member's track record, so a farm can neither dilute nor amplify it. Its ETH is the sum of its members' buys on the curve. Its selectivity is computed on the entity's whole portfolio: forty tokens spread over eight wallets is a forty-token buyer, not eight specialists.

OFFWhen a source is downno link

That rule draws no link at all and those wallets are counted as separate addresses — the honest, conservative answer. The live state of each rule is listed below and never hidden.
