FOMO Radar, rebuilt for degens: a Basic view that says what smart wallets are buying right now, an Advanced view that keeps everything the page has today, and the app credited to its creator, Ironstar Capital.
Why: FOMO Radar is the most used app on /app-usage, but its page is one long wall of tables and paragraphs. A degen wants to know what to buy and who is buying it. Mock, code and data: https://github.com/zakutana/qa-cymetica/tree/4b440dce/outbox/fomo-radar-redesign (README.md first). The pictures use your API as read on 8 Nov, 19:18 UTC.
MUST STAY AS DRAWN: pictures 1 to 5, in their order, colours and wording. The code is ready to use: body.html, fomo.css and fomo.js run on your own /api/v1/clones/fomo-radar/ endpoints unchanged. Every figure is a field you already publish, and nothing is invented.

1. Basic is the default view, and Advanced is one click away. The bar holds the logo, Basic | Advanced, LIVE with the time of the last refresh, and Robinhood | Solana. Both views follow the chain switch, and the page remembers the chosen view (pictures 1 and 3).
2. Basic, top to bottom (picture 1). The headline reads "Memecoin whales. / What they buy. / Right now." (the last line in green), on three lines at any width, phones included. Then one line of lead text with "How it works", which folds out today's intro paragraph and the /duplicate link. Then the market line (RISK-ON and tokens scanned) and the radar, where beam height is conviction, colour shows buy, fresh or exit, a hover shows the token, and a click goes to its card.
3. "Smart wallets are buying": the 12 strongest buy signals as cards, sorted by conviction (picture 4). Each card shows the name, a SAME NAME, HOT or FRESH badge, and a PONS · 1% fee chip where launchpad is pons. The large figure is probability_3x_72h, labelled "chance of 3× in 72h"; your gated and insufficient-history rules apply unchanged. Below it: smart wallets, bought (USD) and time since first buy. Then up to 3 buyers, ordered by leaderboard rank, each with #rank (its place on /traders, or — when it is off the top 25), its address, win % (or its score when off the board), trades, and IN or SOLD; then "+N more". Then "Thin: max buy $X" when thin, and Trade TOKEN ↗, which opens your token page (DexScreener on Solana).
4. Exit signals sit under the cards as red chips, under "Smart money selling", each one linking to its token.
5. Launchpad promotion, the one from /launchpad/compare, told with this board's own count. It appears when PONS tokens are on the Robinhood board: "4× lower fee · N of M tokens on this radar trade on PONS at a 1% fee. EventTrader charges 0.25%: $10,000 of trading costs $25, not $100. Launch free, keep 80% of every fee." It has two buttons: Launch on EventTrader (/launchpad/robinhood) and Compare launchpads (/launchpad/compare) (picture 1).
6. "Best wallets: what they made": the top 4 wallets from /traders (picture 5). Each card shows the realized profit in USD (ETH or SOL at your price), the ETH under it, a win bar, "won A of B trades", and "Buying now:" with the tokens where the wallet is in top_buyers and has not sold. A note says that copying a wallet means buying after it, and that it sells within its average hold. A link opens Advanced.
7. Advanced (picture 3) keeps everything today's page shows, with nothing dropped: the Signals, Accuracy, Top wallets, Clusters and API tabs; the Market, Window and Scanned boxes; Buy signals with All, Hot, Fresh and Exits and every column and row detail; Is the 3× chance real? (current fit, said vs was, hit rate by type, the five steps, misses and gaps, and the $100 backtest); Top wallets; Sibling wallets count once (Robinhood only); and JSON for agents. Every paragraph of today's page stays, folded behind its i or How it works.
8. On a phone (picture 2) the cards are one column, the bar wraps instead of cutting off Solana, and the headline holds its three lines. Your mobile CSS forces every h1 to 28px with wrapping, so this headline needs an exception.
