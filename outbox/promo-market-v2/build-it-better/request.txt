The look of the mockup is binding; the logic stays as you built it for ET-32484. It is built to be copied, not redrawn: every screen reads your live endpoint's own answer with its field names unchanged (data.js is keyed by path; in app.js swap api() for fetch and keep the renders). Where your backend cannot do what is drawn, change as little as it takes and name each change in the BB-SPEC.
One app: /creator-dashboard and /<username> become one page with one header and two tabs, Market and My dashboard, on a centred column like the rest of the site (pictures 1, 4). No second header, no visited-purple link.
Speed: the page sends its shell at once and draws each tab from its own read. Today, signed in, /expire76 sends nothing for 6 to 8 s and then 787 KB, every old tab rendered and hidden. While the reads come back the loader shows: the Promo Market's cube with a real percentage per read and the reads named under it (picture 10).
The cube is the Promo Market's mark, in the header, the market's hero, the empty market and the loader: glass faces with soft glowing edges, standing on one corner, swaying and floating slowly (no spin, no cube inside it), each face a screen showing a product's page; the page's colours only (pictures 1, 2, 10).
Every product picture is the page shot Build it Better already shows (preview_url): the cards, the market, the drawers, Post a brief and the cube (pictures 1, 3, 4, 5).
Market: hero, the three numbers in one row under the buttons, four steps, Open briefs with the three sorts, a designed empty state, For builders and For AI agents side by side, How counted and What this does not do yet in folds (pictures 1, 2).
A brief's card: page shot with the days left and the number of materials, name and link, the owner's line, Up for grabs, only the pays it offers (per post, per new user, per new visitor), a green Take it. Its drawer adds the funds check, the materials to download or copy, and after Take it the promoter's own link and Register post (pictures 1, 3).
One budget for every promoter: several can take one brief, first come first paid, and nothing is counted or owed once it is spent (your budget_spent rule). Say so on the card's Up for grabs, on Post a brief and on the brief's own page.
Post a brief is a page that fits one 1080 screen, three columns: 1 product (page shots) and pay, each price switched on or off; 2 materials for promoters: upload pictures and a video, ready-made texts, links, the first one the picture for posts (the page shot by default); 3 the card as promoters will see it, what the budget buys, the funds note, Post the brief (picture 5). New: an upload route and materials on a brief (README).
A brief's own page for its owner: spent of budget, up for grabs, visitors, new visitors, new users, posts paid; visitors by day; from click to user; promoters with Pay; where visitors came from; not counted with the reasons; materials used; quotes; Pause, Add budget, Edit prices (picture 6). New on /results: clicks, users_by_day, sources, by_promoter, materials_used (README).
My dashboard keeps every section of today's dashboard, one screen per tab, in the new look: My products (picture 4), Today (7), Briefs I took (11), Queues as a list with the thread beside it (8), Journalists (14), Radar apps (12), Kits (13), Pages & links as one sortable table whose rows open their links (9), Rewards (15), Agent key (16), Start an app and a company (17). On Today the post of the day comes first; the agent prompt sits behind Agent prompts, Why post and the steps in a fold.
Real in the mockup: your products, their traffic and every other figure, as your endpoints answered on 2026-10-26. Samples: the briefs and their results behind the corner switch, and the materials.
