The look is binding, exactly: every screen at 1920 x 1080 must look like its picture, and every size, colour, font, radius, spacing, order and animation in the mockup's style.css is part of the design. Copy style.css as it is; each screen is one render function in app.js reading your live endpoint's own answer with field names unchanged (in app.js swap api() for fetch and keep the renders). Please do not redesign, simplify or swap any part; where your backend truly cannot show something as drawn, change as little as it takes and name each change in the BB-SPEC.
The functionality does not change: every route, endpoint, field, rule, check, count, flow, button action and text of today's Promo Market and creator dashboard stays exactly as it is and moves into the new look.
One app: /creator-dashboard and /<username> become one page with one header and two tabs, Market and My dashboard, on a centred column like the rest of the site (spec 2).
Speed: the page sends its shell at once and draws each tab from its own read (today, signed in, /expire76 sends nothing for 6 to 8 s, then 787 KB); while the reads come back, the loader with the cube and a real percentage per read (spec 3, picture 10).
The cube is the Promo Market's mark, in the header, the hero, the empty market and the loader: glass standing on one corner, soft glowing edges (no lines), no back faces showing, no cube inside, swaying and floating (no spin), its faces showing the products' pages (spec 4, pictures 1, 2, 10).
Every product picture is the page shot Build it Better already shows (preview_url), on the cards, the market, the drawers, Post a brief and the cube (pictures 1, 3, 4, 5).
Market as drawn (spec 5, pictures 1, 2): the hero with the three numbers in one row under the buttons and a green See in the Universe button under the cube (to https://cymetica.com/company/app#universe), four steps, Open briefs with the cards, the empty state, builders and AI agents side by side, the two folds. A brief opened in a drawer (spec 6, picture 3).
My dashboard as drawn (spec 7): the rail, one screen per tab: My products (4), Today (7), Briefs I took (11), Queues with the thread beside the list (8), Journalists (14), Radar apps (12), Kits (13), Pages & links as one sortable table (9), Rewards (15), Agent key (16), Start an app and a company (17).
Post a brief is a page that fits one 1080 screen: product and pay, materials for promoters, the card as promoters will see it (spec 8, picture 5). A brief's own page for its owner with its numbers (spec 9, picture 6). One budget for every promoter, first come first paid, nothing counted or owed once it is spent: said on the card, on Post a brief and on the brief's page (your budget_spent rule).
The only new functionality, all on the brief (spec 11): materials (an upload route and materials[] on a brief, the first one the picture for posts), the brief page's fields on /results (clicks, users_by_day, sources, by_promoter, materials_used), and preview_url on products and briefs.
Real in the mockup: your products, their traffic and every other figure, as your endpoints answered on 2026-10-26. Samples: the briefs, their results and their materials behind the corner switch.
