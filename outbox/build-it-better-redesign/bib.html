How it works

  1. You name the app and what is wrong with it.
  2. We answer with the scope, including what we will not build.
  3. You lock the scope, or ask for a change.
  4. It goes live on EventTrader. The clock shows how long it really took.
  5. You test it and say pass or fail. A fail goes back to the queue.
  6. Prove it: run your original bot against the rebuild head-to-head in paper, same market and window, and share the card. cymetica.com/h2h

What gets built, and what does not

Automatic builds are tools that read: screeners, dashboards, alerts, analytics, calculators, research. Anything that moves money (deposits, withdrawals, placing orders, signing) is reviewed by a person before it ships. Every build says plainly what it does not do, and carries our own name and branding, never the original's.
