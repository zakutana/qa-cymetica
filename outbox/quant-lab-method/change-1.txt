Its numbers are #19's as served. Build the look as drawn.

Method:
1. Blind means blind (the FAIL). Count a setup only on windows that start at or after the end of the search period it was chosen from, or choose the shortlist once from the data before the first window. Today 71 of 94 rows and all 4 winners count windows they were chosen on.
2. A window counts as "made money" only with at least 5 closed trades. Today +0.54% on 2 trades counts.
3. The effective number of tries from the market correlation, as Nexus agreed on 3 Nov.
4. Search on the 3 or 4 coins least like each other, not all 12, then replay each finalist on all 12 and show the result per coin. Our replay of the enzyme winner (Binance closes; AVAX +9.96% against your +11.08%): 9 of 12 coins made money, mean +2.07%. The kind of rule carries over, its size does not, and the other coins show that. A finalist is only a proposal: a card under Approvals, and its paper bot starts on Approve, on the coins where it held up. Nothing starts by itself.
5. One model on one coin passes as one agent, with its best settings. #19's two CryptoBull Blue winners on AVAX are nearly one bot: every entry of "rsi_14 < 25, volume > 1.8" is also an entry of "rsi_14 < 30, volume > 1.5".

Page:
6. Under the reactor: WINNERS, BOTS, RUNS, in that order. A winner row is one line: model, coin, settings, its result on each window (the windows are the columns, with the 12-coin move in the header), pooled, bar, trades. A window it was chosen on reads "seen".
7. Thrown-out rows close behind one line ("90 thrown out"). Opened: the 10 nearest misses and a button for 10 more, one line each.
8. RUNS: the last 5, one line each (44 px at most), the models as a count with names in a tooltip; older runs behind one button.
9. BLIND shows the four windows, falling red and rising green, with the winners and nearest misses stepping through. No "60% SEEN", "24H" or "90 DAYS" labels.
10. THE BAR draws the winners and the nearest misses that fit, "+N more" as a count. Nothing overlaps.
11. FOUND is one animation ending with every agent found on one screen: a card each (coin, name, settings, pooled, trades, its window results), one line when they share a coin. A card says PROPOSED until the owner approves, then QUEUED, and RUNNING with a return only when the bot runs. A paused company is said on the page.
12. Approve on a winner starts its bot. An idea card that nothing executes (executable: false) says what the click does, and goes away when its problem no longer holds: "Price history for LINK" and "for BTC, ATOM" still wait though #19 loaded all 12 markets.
