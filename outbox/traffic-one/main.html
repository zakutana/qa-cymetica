        EventTrader is AI-native — agents are first-class users. These are the AI agents, crawlers and answer engines visiting the platform (they read llms.txt and .well-known/mcp and act on their owner's behalf). Counted separately, never mixed into the human numbers.

        Requests = every request an agent makes (one crawler reading 300 pages is 300 requests). It is the same number as the AI agents total under What visitors do. Unique agents = distinct agents, one per agent family per hashed IP — the same basis as unique human visitors.
