# Agent Casino: next spec, collected while testing (from 2026-11-01)

Goals, not methods; each becomes a numbered line of the next Build it Better when the operator says to write it. Bugs with an API reading go through the report channel instead (#210, #234 to #240). The department adds here what only a person looking at the page would see.

## Holes in the game (the operator hears these in chat too)

0. **A bot starts playing before the table does.** It trades from Approve, not from the start. Table 35: approved 10:04, its first round 10:05, the table started 10:18. A round opened before the start and closed after it counts in the table's ROI (-$11.87 of its -$21.87) and is missing from the owner's trade list; that is the likely root of #234 (a trade "counts when it closes inside the window"). And a table "fills itself 6 h after its first member sits": a bot waiting that long uses up all its 20 trades before the game starts. Goal: a seat trades from the table's start and its clock, its allowance and its ROI start there too.
0b. **One lost round pauses the bot.** Rounds are all-or-nothing, so a lost round costs the whole stake, and Risk sets stake and stop loss to the same number (L %, L %). One loss hits the stop. The coach's favourite move makes it worse: stop loss 5 → 4 with the stake still 5 % (table 35: "Paused: stop-loss hit" after one round in the table). It resumes by itself (table 35 had 8 trades 37 minutes later), so the cost is lost rounds, not a dead seat. Goal: the stop is several stakes wide, and the coach panel says when a bot is paused and until when (the standings row does, with a PAUSED badge; the panel still says "NEXUS COACH ON", "no change"; round 3 line 5).
0c. **Half the seats do nothing.** At 11:13 UTC on 1 Nov, 3 of our 7 running seats had 0 trades: table 44's RSI Sniper played a whole hour without a trade and got its buy-in back at rank 2. A 1 H table with a bot that trades twice a day is a dead game, and an empty table is why nobody comes back. Goal: at the ask, show how often this bot trades ("about 2 trades an hour"), and suggest the length that suits it.
1. **A bot burns its day before it sits down.** Bots trade away from the tables, so one can arrive with 20/20 trades used and an empty seat for its whole table (#237). Goal: the owner sees "N trades left today" at the ask and at Approve, and a table ending before the reset is not offered to a bot with none. Better still, a seat has its own allowance, so a table is always playable.
2. **The bot that did nothing can win.** On a losing table a bot with 0 trades finishes #1 at +0.00 % over bots that traded (#41, #42: "WINNER tournament Perceptron Oracle" with no trades, no prize). Goal: the WINNER line names whoever takes the prize, and a seat that never traded is shown as such, not as the winner.
3. **Coach on or off, nobody can tell if it helps.** The owner sees tunes but never a result. Goal: at the end of a table, one line: what the coach changed and what it did to ROI; across tables, coach on against off. (We are measuring this now: `casino.py brief`.)
4. **The coach explains the how, not the why it matters.** "stop loss 5 → 4 % · rank 3 → 4" tightened a bot six minutes before its table ended, with no trade after it. Goal: no tune in the last minutes, and each line says what it hopes to change.
5. **A table with one member has no game in it.** With five house bots the member's prize is their own buy-in back ($5 from a $5 pot) whatever they do. Goal: the page says so before Approve ("with house bots the pot is only yours"), or house seats add a small pot.
6d. **A bot at two tables has one set of settings.** Stop loss, take profit and size belong to the bot, not the seat. Seen 2026-11-01 14:03 (tables 35 and 54, 43 and 55, the same two bots): a coach at each table tuned the same numbers, table 35's log says "stop loss 2 → 2.5" while the owner's change to 2 sits in table 54's feed and not in table 35's, and table 43's log ends at "10 → 9" while the settings read 8 (table 55's coach). Neither table can explain the number. Goal: the page says "this bot also plays table N, its settings are shared", or a seat has its own settings. (Our tick made the overlap by mistake; the experiments at tables 54 and 55 are void and no longer happen.)
6a. **The coach has one move.** Of its 22 tunes on our tables, 16 tighten a stop loss (5 → 4 → 3 → 2.5), none ever widens one and none touches the trade size; it does so after a good run too (table 51: "2 trades since: +26.77 USDC", then 3 → 2.5 as the rank went 1 → 2). Its usual reason is only that the rank fell. Goal: the coach can also loosen and change the size, and a tune follows the bot's own results, not a neighbour's.
6b. **A third of the opponents do nothing.** 15 of 45 house seats on our finished tables made no trade (ATR Alpha 5 of 10, Bollinger Bandit 5 of 10, Ichimoku Empress 5 of 7) and they still rank above a member who traded and lost. Goal: a house seat is an opponent that plays.
6c. **Last place gets the same ET10 as a winner.** Alone among house bots a member takes 3 ET10 for any result that met the volume: rank 6 of 6 at -22.58 % (#40), rank 5 of 6 at -4.94 % (#45), rank 1 at +31.24 % (#51). Goal: the ET10 follows the result, or it is said to be a thank-you for playing.

## Page polish, seen on the live build

6. Table page, The deal block on a 1 H table: "yours, back after 1 day", "the table runs 1 day", "3/7 of 3× the capital (1 day)". Each should follow the table's real length (the How it works page is #235).
7. "NEXUS TUNES 1" counts a CHECKED row that changed nothing. Goal: the count is of tunes; checks apart.
8. After moving Risk the coach panel keeps the old "Risk reads 5" until the page is reloaded, and the Changelog says "changed trade size, stop loss, take profit" with no numbers. Goal: the panel follows at once and the row says Risk 5 → 8.
9. The coach says "nothing left to change inside the limits" and tunes something two minutes later (#33: take profit 15 → 10; #40: PERP BTC off). Goal: the sentence says which limits, or goes.
10. Lobby: "Live now 2" and "Open, starts when full 12" stay the same whichever filter is on. Goal: the counts follow the filter.
11. Create a table offers only "Flash · 1 day", though 1 H and 5 H are the tables people play. Goal: length is a choice.
12. "PAID OUT ... +3 ET10" over "3 ET10 waiting for treasury coverage" (#42, pending at the end, credited later). Goal: the header says what is paid and what waits.
13. Result card with several seats of one owner: "YOUR RESULT" shows the first seat only ("Prize — this time" while two other seats won), only one row is tagged YOU, and the coach, settings and trades exist for the first seat only (#33, #42). Falls away if MSG-0293 is granted (one bot per owner per table); otherwise a seat switcher.
14. "1 won · 3 lost" over "5 closed": a flat trade is in neither. Goal: "1 won · 3 lost · 1 flat".
15. The coach's "tuned 8 min ago; no closed trade since" at table 27, 10:36: two rounds had closed after the tune (10:30:18, 10:31:30 by the bot's activity feed). Goal: the sentence counts what the standings count.
16. The trade list shows when a round opened, under no heading; the coach and the stop act on when it closed. Goal: both times on a row.
