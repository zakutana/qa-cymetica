Gem Screener, Top Picks and the coin panel: what a reader needs to trust a pick before buying it, on the screen where they decide. No figure is computed differently and no gate changes.
Why: today a pick that had an $8.5M exploit nine months ago, or passes Liquid on exchange volume with 7% slip in its pool, reads the same as any other; and a near miss shows no figures until it is clicked.
