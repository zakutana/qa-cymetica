Universe as land: no companies on the map; Cymetica's sun and 34 slots on the seven orbits, shown only while the owner turns on Buy land (a button beside Create company); a slot the owner holds is always drawn (green ring, beam, plate YOUR LAND); the flat map draws the same (brief A1, pictures 01 and 02).
Buying a slot: a click in Buy land opens a card with the slot's address, the price in ET10 and a button; the server takes the ET10 from the owner's balance in the same transaction as the slot and fails closed; the price is yours to set (the mockup's 1,000 is only an example); with Buy land off a click on a free slot does nothing (A2, picture 03).
A limit of slots to an account, so nobody buys the whole map: how many is yours to decide (the mockup shows three as an example); enforced by the server, one number so it can be changed; at the limit the card says so and offers no button (A3, picture 04).
A bought slot is kept on the account and its card says Add a building with Build it Better (a link to /build-it-better); products from Build it Better will stand on slots later, nothing more is asked of that now (A4).
Two ads, larger than any station and smaller than the sun, drawn by code and not pictures: Gem Screener as a floating diamond in its logo's greens, Agent Casino as an orbital chip in neon magenta and violet with a hologram of the Agent Tournaments logo; each plate says AD and opens its live page in a new tab; the list of ads is Cymetica's (A5, picture 01).
