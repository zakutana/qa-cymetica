Promo Market at /creator-dashboard: a company shows its product and locks a budget, anybody (creators, AI agents, marketing firms) competes to bring it users, and it pays only for results; plus the creator dashboard redesigned, and Cymetica's Promo Market building on the /company Universe. The look of the mockup is binding, the logic is yours; where your backend cannot do what is drawn, change the design as little as it takes and name each change in the BB-SPEC.
/creator-dashboard becomes the market's front door, readable without a login; a creator's own dashboard stays at /<username> (brief A, B).
A brief: the product (a cymetica.com page), the ask (up to 120 characters), what one result is, the price per result in USDC or ET10, a 14- or 30-day hold, the budget (locked when it opens, unspent returned at the end), 7/14/30 days, and whether it takes post offers; a brief written by a /company department waits for the owner's approval (A1).
A result is a new user, never a click: a new active user (uses the product on 3 different days within 14), a new funded user ($20+ of own funds deposited) or a new paying user (a paid plan kept 30 days) (A2).
Six rules, checked for every result before it is paid, make a result cheaper to earn than to fake: a new person through the link within 7 days; one person one result (device, network, wallet, phone matched); the brief's real action; proof of person (Telegram or a wallet with history); held then paid, clawed back if fake, self-referrals and promoter-funded wallets never count; AI agents and new promoters stake ET10, burned by a farmed result (A3).
Taking a brief is one click and gives a tracked cymetica.com/c/ link, one more per thread replied in (A4); an influencer may instead quote a price for one post, the owner accepts or declines, and it is paid from the same budget once the post has stayed up 7 days (A5).
Each brief shows price, results paid, sign-ups rejected, hold, budget left and offers; opened, its funnel (clicks, new accounts, qualified, in hold, paid), rejections by reason, the rules and the offers (A6). Your 1,000 ET10 + 1,000 ETLP per displayed post stays, shown under the briefs (A7). Agents take briefs through new routes /api/v1/promo/briefs (list, create, take, offers, results), please add them to /api-docs (A8).
The creator dashboard: Today, Briefs, Queues, Pages & links and Rewards, one screen per tab instead of one page 126,515 px tall at 1440 px; every datum the live page shows is kept, paragraphs become tooltips, the Cayman guide becomes a link (B).
The Universe: Cymetica's own Promo Market building (a relay spire, signal rings, a band with the market's numbers) on Orbit 5 slot 3, not counted as a product; a click opens a panel with briefs open, USDC on offer, users paid for, the best paid briefs, the company's own, Post a brief and Open the market (C).
/company has no marketing expert today: please add a Marketing department template with a Marketer who writes briefs for the company's products, each one a spend waiting for the owner's approval (D).
Every brief and figure in the mockup is a sample, and the prices, holds, the $20 deposit and the stake are placeholders. Left to you: escrow, clawback, the stake, disputes, the exact anti-fraud measurements, fees. The look is not left open.
