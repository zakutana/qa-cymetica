/creator-dashboard becomes the market's front door for everyone not logged in, readable without an account; a logged-in creator still lands on their own dashboard at /<username>, as today, so your daily "see the impact of your posts" link keeps opening on their clicks, reads and referred visitors (Promote · Today; an account with products of its own lands on My products) (brief A, B).
A brief pays a promoter two things in USDC: per post (one that stays up 7 days, confirmed by your display checker) and per new user its link brings (after the checks below); Up for grabs is what is left of the budget (A1).
A brief's card shows only the product's name and link, Up for grabs, per post, per new user, the days left and Take it; the page shows three numbers (USDC up for grabs, open briefs, top pay per new user) and a four-step tutorial (A2, A3; picture 1).
Taking a brief is one click and gives the promoter its own tracked cymetica.com/c/ link; opened, a brief shows how to earn, one line to say, the threads asking for it, and two folds: how a new user is counted, and a price quote for a post from a big audience (A4; picture 2). Ideally a promoter needs no account at all: paste only a wallet address (for example on Base), get the link, and the rewards arrive in that wallet; whether that is possible and how to make it safe is your call, otherwise the lightest sign-up you have (A4b).
Only real new users are paid, and how to make sure is yours: promoters, AI agents above all, will try to farm the pay per new user with fake accounts, so please verify what really works on your platform and choose the best protection, the only rule being that farming must cost more than it pays; the six checks drawn in the mockup's fold are examples, not a requirement, and the fold sits at the bottom, never on top (A5).
New routes /api/v1/promo/briefs (list, create, take, quotes, results), please add them to /api-docs (A6).
The dashboard: My products lists every app the account built with Build it Better with its 30-day traffic; click one, create a brief (per post, per new user, budget, 7/14/30 days), done; then four numbers, a chart of the visitors the briefs brought per day, and per product what it brought; Promote keeps every action /creator-dashboard has today (Today, Briefs I took, Queues with regenerate, mark posted and channel posts, Pages & links, Rewards, register your own post, the agent key), one screen per tab (B; pictures 3 to 6).
The Universe: Cymetica's own Promo Market building on Orbit 5 slot 3, a billboard of light: a cyan spire with a ticker band projecting a holographic cube of four ad screens (magenta and amber crossfading, scan lines, flicker, glowing edges, a sweeping scan plane), three holographic ad cards circling it and two searchlights; a click opens a panel that says two things: for promoters, earn USDC (up for grabs, the best paid per post and per new user); for builders, have a product, get users (what your briefs brought), with Earn: take a brief and Promote my product (C; pictures 7 to 9).
/company has no marketing expert today: please add a Marketing department template with a Marketer who writes briefs for the company's products, each one a spend waiting for the owner's approval (D).
Every brief and figure in the mockup is a sample and the prices are placeholders; real are the owner's apps and their traffic. Left to you: the protection against fake users (A5), the no-account path (A4b), escrow and payouts, quotes, disputes, fees. The look is not left open.
