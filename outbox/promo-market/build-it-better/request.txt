Promo Market at /creator-dashboard: an owner shows a product and sets a budget, anybody (creators, AI agents, marketing firms) competes to promote it, paid in USDC per post and per new user; plus the dashboard as My products and Promote, and Cymetica's Promo Market building on the /company Universe. The look of the mockup is binding, the logic is yours; where your backend cannot do what is drawn, change the design as little as it takes and name each change in the BB-SPEC.
/creator-dashboard becomes the market's front door, readable without a login; a creator's own dashboard stays at /<username> (brief A, B).
A brief pays a promoter two things in USDC: per post (one that stays up 7 days, confirmed by your display checker) and per new user its link brings (after the checks below); Up for grabs is what is left of the budget (A1).
A brief's card shows only the product's name and link, Up for grabs, per post, per new user, the days left and Take it; the page shows three numbers (USDC up for grabs, open briefs, top pay per new user) and a four-step tutorial (A2, A3).
Taking a brief is one click and gives the promoter its own tracked cymetica.com/c/ link; opened, a brief shows how to earn, one line to say, the threads asking for it, and two folds: how a new user is counted, and a price quote for a post from a big audience (A4).
A new user is counted only if it is a new person through the link within 7 days, one person one user (device, network, wallet, phone matched), uses the product on 3 different days within 14, has proof of person (Telegram or a wallet with history), and survives a 14-day check with clawback; AI agents and new promoters stake ET10, burned by a farmed user. Folded at the bottom, never on top (A5).
New routes /api/v1/promo/briefs (list, create, take, quotes, results), please add them to /api-docs (A6).
The dashboard: My products lists every app the account built with Build it Better with its 30-day traffic; click one, create a brief (per post, per new user, budget, 7/14/30 days), done; then four numbers, a chart of the visitors the briefs brought per day, and per product what it brought; Promote keeps everything /creator-dashboard does today (Today, Briefs I took, Queues, Pages & links, Rewards), one screen per tab (B).
The Universe: Cymetica's own Promo Market building on Orbit 5 slot 3, a billboard of light: a cyan spire with a ticker band projecting a holographic cube of four ad screens (magenta and amber crossfading, scan lines, flicker, glowing edges, a sweeping scan plane), three holographic ad cards circling it and two searchlights; a click opens a panel with what is up for grabs, the best briefs, your products' results, Post a brief and Open the market (C).
/company has no marketing expert today: please add a Marketing department template with a Marketer who writes briefs for the company's products, each one a spend waiting for the owner's approval (D).
Every brief and figure in the mockup is a sample and the prices, checks and stake are placeholders; real are the owner's apps and their traffic. Left to you: escrow, clawback, the stake, quotes, disputes, the exact anti-fraud measurements, fees. The look is not left open.
