Before you commit a week to either

  • Who owns the code? Both want a code link. Build it Better produces a page on cymetica.com, so ask Cymetica whether you receive the source before you enter.
  • Your brief is public. Requests on the Build it Better board can be read without a key. Do not put anything in a request that you want to keep private.
  • Start early. Delivery time has varied. Do not plan around the last day.
  • Access. At the time of writing, filing a Build it Better request needs a paid Nexus plan (Pro is $20 a month). The free plan cannot file.

Left out, and why

  • Arbitrum Open House Dubai: the entry must be deployed on Arbitrum One or an Orbit chain, which a web page cannot do.
  • ETHGlobal Mumbai, TUM Munich, HackMeridian Lisbon, Encode London: in person only.
  • Decentralize AI: you submit an article, not a product.
  • QIE Hackathon 3.0: its rules ban AI-generated projects.
