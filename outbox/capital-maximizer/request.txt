New product: Capital Maximizer. Everything is at https://github.com/zakutana/qa-cymetica/tree/334881af/outbox/capital-maximizer : the full spec (SPEC.md), the working mockup (open mock.html), the target pictures in pictures/ (01-risk to 13-logo; "picture N" below means these) and our platform research in research/. The design is a proposal for the look only: every number in it is made up and every text is an example, so write your own copy everywhere. Build the layout, parts, colours and motion exactly as drawn.
The idea: the user puts in USDC and picks a risk (Conservative, Medium, High Risk, OG Gambler). Cymetica's engine finds the best strategies across all of DeFi and every chain. The user picks one of the top 3, starts it, watches it in Positions, and can exit any time back to their wallet.
1. The engine is the product. Build a top engine that never stops searching for the best possible strategies for all four risk tiers, across all of DeFi and every chain. It must be unbeatable and always up to date: rates, platforms, chains and strategies refreshed continuously, the most modern strategies adopted as they appear, and your own algorithms and strategies welcome. You are AI-native: the user only picks a risk and an amount and relies on you for the rest. Top quality only.
2. Cross-chain. One strategy may run its legs on several chains; the mockup moves USDC with Circle CCTP as the example. Show the whole path everywhere, e.g. Ethereum → Arbitrum.
3. Platforms and chains are your call. research/ hands over our review: 62 platforms on 21 chains, the strategy map with the data feeds each strategy needs, what we would leave out and why, and sources for every platform. Use it as input. Decide what goes in, build your own verification layer that decides which platforms pass and which do not, and add your own platforms and chains.
4. One hard rule: no KYC platform, ever. That means no venue, pool or token that asks the user, or the product on the user's behalf, for identity. Our research found Falcon and Solstice failing this.
5. Header (all pictures): the logo (picture 13, logos/logo-12.svg), CAPITAL MAXIMIZER over BY CYMETICA, and the tabs Maximize, Positions (with the open count) and Platforms (with the count). Colours: a profit is always green; red is only for a loss, liquidation and EXIT.
6. Maximize: four steps on a rail of hexagon nodes. Risk (picture 1): four tier cards, each with an emblem, a risk meter, a big max profit per month, the leverage and the assets. Amount (picture 2): one big USDC input, quick amounts, the max profit this month, and Find top 3. Finding (picture 3): a radar of platform logos with live counters.
7. Top 3 (pictures 4-6): three cards side by side. Each is a skill tree flowing top to bottom, from the USDC root through hexagon nodes to the max-profit apex: protocol logos with rate plates, a CCTP node when the route leaves the home chain, and a leverage diamond with − and +. Fine lines carry light along them, nodes grow in on reveal, and step numbers match a numbered step list with the user's own amounts. Footer: max profit in $, return, leverage, liquidation, the chain path, Select. − and + re-compute the card.
8. Plan (picture 7): the picked tree laid out left to right with its steps beside it, then a leverage slider, liquidation, max profit and START.
9. Positions (pictures 8-11): per route, one big IN THE POOL number, the result in $ and %, a value line, a liquidation gauge showing how far the asset can fall (a distance, never written with a minus sign) and a big red EXIT. EXIT asks first (picture 9). Then the route's own tree plays backwards: nodes empty, links drain, and the light flows back to the root, which is the wallet, while the pool counts down and the wallet counts up, under a progress panel (picture 10). It ends on what is back in the wallet (picture 11).
10. Platforms (picture 12): one small tile per platform (logo, name, chains, kind, TVL, and the check of your verification layer), filters by kind and chain, about 50 tiles on one screen.
11. Numbers are all yours, from live data and one model. One strategy shows the same number on every screen (tier card, amount, top 3 card, plan, the position at start), and tuning the leverage re-computes everything.
Done when: every screen matches its picture, with your own texts; the engine runs continuously for all four tiers and shows the top 3 per tier across chains; no KYC platform anywhere; the Platforms tab shows your verified set; the same number shows everywhere; and EXIT unwinds into the wallet.
