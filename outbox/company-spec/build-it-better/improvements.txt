The code and every screen are in the same folder, pinned to that commit.
The design is the template: use its code (it is finished and performance-tuned, design/PERF-NOTES.md) and connect your logic to it. design/sim.js only simulates a backend; replace it with yours. Where your backend cannot do what the design shows, change the design as little as it takes and name each change in the BB-SPEC; everywhere else keep it as drawn (brief section 0).
Every text is a draft written from the owner's side: check each claim against your backend and rewrite what is wrong.
Colours: the page's interface keeps the site palette; the 3D office and the Universe are a game scene outside it (the orange "needs you" glow and the eight station colours are part of the design).
NEXUS everywhere instead of "Manager"; its room is Command; the money room is the Vault (section 0).
Standalone mode, as the Gem Screener: /company/app is the app alone without the site menu (the mockup is this view), /company the same app under the site's header. Today /company/app is 404 (section 1).
Entry for someone with no company: the landing (headline, two tiles Start a company / Explore the Universe, Cymetica's sun with companies orbiting it), the two-step builder (Name & look with seven stations and eight colours; Start: New or CyMetica Pre-Build) and the click-through tour of the pre-built office, one bubble per room (section 2).
Creating a company should not be free, or anyone can create thousands. Your call: Pro plan and up only, a price in ET10 (for example 5,000), or a 7-day trial. The mockup shows the step as a popup on Create company (signed out: sign up first); keep the look when you add it (section 3).
NEXUS builds the whole company from one message: departments, charters, experts, processes between them and each room's link. The CyMetica Pre-Build is exactly that request; NEXUS must be able to build it from nothing: "Make me a company with a QA department, where every bug that is logged and verified earns me ET10; a Development department, where new apps and ideas are built with my approval; Operations, which audits the processes and keeps them optimised; a Quant Lab, where experiments test ways to make money on Cymetica; and a Trading desk, where I run the ones that work, even with real money." How it works behind the scenes is yours to design (section 4).
The office: the 3D office (WebGPU, WebGL2 fallback) and the 2D office one click away, replacing the 3D network view; fixed rooms Command, Vault (ET10 and USDC piles, always private) and Operations (weekly audit, keeps processes optimised); a room glows orange only while it waits on its owner (section 5).
Every room links to its page: Cymetica QA to /leaderboard/bugs?filter=mine, which must open My Bugs with every bug this department filed (today the page reads nothing from its address), Development and Design studio to /leaderboard/features, Quant Lab to the new Experiments page, Trading desk to My bots (/my-agents) and Agent Tournaments (section 5).
Cymetica QA files every verified bug straight into the tracker under the owner's hunter name and wallet, reopens known bugs instead of filing twice, re-tests claimed fixes; every bug it files shows in the owner's My Bugs; its ET10 is lit on the department and in the Vault (section 6).
Quant Lab finds the ways to make money: strategies worked out with NEXUS, proven on paper with the success rule and window set first. Trading desk puts its winning bots into production: an Agent Tournaments seat or a small real-money stake, each after the owner's approval; the company never trades itself (section 6).
New Experiments page, the Quant Lab's: one card per experiment (return, line, bots, instructions), opened with ranked bots, Run / Pause, add and remove bots, rename, edit instructions, + New experiment (section 7, design/experiments.html).
Processes tab: the owner writes how the company works as one sentence each ("When a bug is found anywhere, Cymetica QA verifies it and files it"); NEXUS reads trigger, department, work and review, says what is missing, never duplicates; each shows a "then" line. Today departments cannot hand work to each other. Please review it as a design and keep it if sound (section 8).
Universe: every company as a station on Cymetica's orbits, public ones open read-only, private ones locked (name, station, mission). No sample companies: only companies people created. A green Create company on the map. Today /api/v1/companies lists Investable ones only, none today (section 9).
Portfolio: the account's Build it Better apps with your traffic numbers; ET10 per new unique user and a USDC share per use are proposals, your rates (section 10).
Per department: today's spend, cost of a turn, a daily cap in dollars the owner sets (section 11). Public / private is its own switch in Settings, apart from Investable (section 12).
Every function documented for agents and usable through the API and MCP: create, departments, processes, NEXUS, approvals, experiments, Vault, Settings. New tab Connect AI: "Talk to NEXUS. Run your whole company from your favourite AI": the key, the MCP connector and one ready prompt for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI. Please design this tab yourselves; ours is a sketch (section 13).
Left to you: the price, how NEXUS and processes work behind the scenes, the copy, and any design change your backend forces (named). The look is not left open.
