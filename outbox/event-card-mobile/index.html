On 25 Sep 2026 Novo Nordisk (Wegovy, Ozempic) licensed Nanexa's PharmaShell for up to five monthly/quarterly injectable programs — €615M upfront plus development and regulatory milestones, €1.165B in total; Nanexa's shares rose ~150%. PharmaShell grows an ultra-thin inorganic shell around each drug particle by atomic layer deposition, and Nanexa says that shell is primarily aluminium oxide. Novo is the buyer, so the move is not in Novo — it is in the inorganic-coating supply chain.
