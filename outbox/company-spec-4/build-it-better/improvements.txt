The look of the mockup is binding, the logic is yours; where your backend cannot do what is drawn, change the design as little as it takes and name each change in the BB-SPEC. Where the mockup still shows dollars, A1 wins: units.
Units, not dollars: per department cost_usd_7d equals units_7d and the plan's windows limit the work, so "$2.13 today" says the owner pays when he does not; show units wherever spend is shown (department rows, the Spend card, the cap) with the window beside them ("0.6 of 5 in the 5-hour window"); USDC only for extra usage (A1).
Units per task, on the Tasks tab, in a department's room and in the task's detail, sortable; served already in tasks[].units and turn_strip[]; a task with no units yet says "not measured", not zero (A2).
The Products tab lists only what the owner created: a page that existed before his first request and that he only improved (My Agents, Events) is not his, round 2's rule from your dates; no card for a request with no page (Usage, Creator rewards, Airdrop Farmer while building) (A3).
The Supervisor in two layers (C). Engine layer, in the background: as your Telegram bot improved from its chats, it follows every company's NEXUS chats, tasks, outcomes and what owners approved, rejected and complained about, and keeps improving the engine; owners never see it and it files nothing under their names. Company layer, in the Audit tab: a review of that company's processes, charters and setup that applies safe changes itself (each listed, undone in one click) and leaves what spends units or money, or changes departments, to the owner as an approval; each finding links its message or task.
How the company runs (E). On Max 5x the 5-hour window ran out after 11 turns in 23 minutes (8 were Manager chat answers) and blocked the company three hours: make a window last a working session, a chat turn cost a fraction of a department turn, and queued work not wait for the next pass. That pulls against the countdown we drew, which counts passes and turns, so how it runs is yours to decide. With passes and turns: one place at the foot of the office, in the bar of the plan's windows: a cyan ring that empties, "next pass 0:12", "2/40 turns", "running now", "paused" or "waiting for the window · 14:05". Without them: the same place with only "running now", "waiting for the window" and "paused".
Play and pause per department: a round switch in its colour on its row (Command has none); its tasks wait, a running turn finishes, the others go on; the row says "Paused by you" or "Paused: the company is paused". A paused room goes dark in the office once its last turn ends (lights out, "Paused" on its wall); the whole company paused darkens every department, Command stays lit (F).
The revenue share (H) is a sketch only: how it works is yours to decide (shares, paid places, floor, payout, what counts as revenue brought); we draw the look: every company ranked by the revenue it brought Cymetica in 30 days, the top 3 sharing up to 5% of Cymetica's revenue (5% / 3% / 1%, a $10,000 floor, monthly USDC are placeholders). Our one suggestion: never count the owner's own or linked accounts, never pay a company more than it brought.
