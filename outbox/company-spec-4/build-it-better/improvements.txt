The look of the mockup is binding, the logic is yours; where your backend cannot do what is drawn, change the design as little as it takes and name each change in the BB-SPEC. Where the mockup still shows dollars, A1 wins: units.
Units, not dollars: per department cost_usd_7d equals units_7d (Cymetica QA 3.3249 / 3.324890) and the plan's windows are what limit the work, so "$2.13 today" says the owner pays when he does not; show units wherever spend is shown (department rows, the Spend card, the cap) with the window beside them ("0.6 of 5 in the 5-hour window, 8.6 of 30 this week"); USDC only for extra usage (A1).
Units per task, on the Tasks tab, in a department's room and in the task's detail, sortable; served already in tasks[].units and turn_strip[]; a task with no units yet says "not measured", not zero (A2).
The Products tab lists only what the owner created: a page that existed before his first request and that he only improved (My Agents, Events) is not his, apply round 2's rule from your dates; and no card for a request with no page (Usage, Creator rewards, Build it Better for crypto communities, Airdrop Farmer while building) (A3).
The Supervisor in two layers (C). The engine layer, in the background: as your Telegram bot improved from its chats, the Supervisor follows every chat with NEXUS in every company, the tasks and their outcomes and what owners approved, rejected and complained about, and keeps improving the engine that runs all companies; owners never see it, it is in no audit log and files nothing under their names. The company layer, in the Audit tab: a review of that one company that optimizes its processes, charters and setup, applies safe changes itself (each listed, undone with one click) and leaves anything that spends units or money, or adds or removes a department, to the owner as an approval; each finding links the message or task it came from.
The chat, if not already fixed from our bug reports (MSG-0211, MSG-0213): a bubble in the thread while NEXUS works, with the queued task's number; every reply says which message it answers; a task number in a message links to that task (D).
A countdown to the next pass in one place only, at the foot of the office first in the bar of the plan's windows: a cyan ring that empties, "next pass 0:12", "2/40 turns"; green "running now" while a turn runs; grey "paused" or "waiting for the window · 14:05"; from heartbeat.next_pass_at, pass_every_s and run_state.turns_today / turns_cap (E).
Play and pause for each department: a round switch in its colour on its row (Command has none); its tasks wait, a running turn finishes, the others go on; the row says "Paused by you" or "Paused: the company is paused"; it survives a reload. A paused room goes dark in the office once its last turn ends: lamp, neon and floor light out, its name dimmed, "Paused" on its wall; the whole company paused darkens every department, Command stays lit (F).
The revenue share (H): every company ranked by the fees it brought Cymetica in 30 days, the top 3 get a share of every fee on Cymetica. Fees brought: what Cymetica earned from the users who came through the company's products or links and from all volume run through its products; not counted: fees from the owner's own or linked accounts, and bounties or prizes paid to it; we propose a cap, a share never more than the fees the company brought, so trading with yourself can never pay. The numbers in the mockup (5% / 3% / 1%, a $10,000 floor, paid monthly in USDC) are an example for the design only: set your own terms, who gets how many percent and what it takes to qualify. Drawn: a frosted-glass board turned away on its left edge over the Universe's top-left (no boxes, only type and light), open seats that show what they pay and what it takes ("All 3 seats are open. Be the first."), the full leaderboard in the same glass with "Who gets a seat" big on top and a line at the floor, a cyan dollar pulse from the sun to the three, and the first look lower and further left so the board covers open space.
A station on the map looks washed in its colour while the Settings preview shows it right (FDS: steel and green lights in Settings, pale mint all over on the map); our mockup does the same and we could not find why (lights, tone mapping, grade, halo, sky, bloom and angle ruled out); please find it and tell us (I).
The owner writes the line his product shows in the Universe: "In the Universe" on each Products card with Edit (70 characters); only that line shows in the map tooltip and the product's panel, none until he writes one; Cymetica's own buildings keep theirs (J).
Airdrop Farmer on our third slot, Orbit 5 · slot 5 (taken in GET /company/land): its logo as a robot's head of polished gold under a straw hat, amber lights round the face, warm white eyes in bronze bezels that blink, bronze bolts at the sides, a lime-and-gold sprout; never a face in a skin tone; its panel shows the live page's 23 farms, 1 USDC deposit, 10% of realized profit and Visit (K).
The MADE BY plate in a product's panel as the mockup draws it, all in the company's colour: the pill, riveted dark glass, a small arrow under it and a line out of either side (the live one lacks both); Cymetica's own buildings say MADE BY · CYMETICA in its cyan, not a link (L).
