The look of the mockup is binding, the logic is yours; where your backend cannot do what is drawn, change the design as little as it takes and name each change in the BB-SPEC. Where the mockup still shows dollars, A1 wins: units.
Units, not dollars: per department cost_usd_7d equals units_7d (Cymetica QA 3.3249 / 3.324890) and the plan's windows limit the work, so "$2.13 today" says the owner pays when he does not; show units wherever spend is shown (department rows, the Spend card, the cap) with the window beside them ("0.6 of 5 in the 5-hour window, 8.6 of 30 this week"); USDC only for extra usage (A1).
Units per task, on the Tasks tab, in a department's room and in the task's detail, sortable; served already in tasks[].units and turn_strip[]; a task with no units yet says "not measured", not zero (A2).
The Products tab lists only what the owner created: a page that existed before his first request and that he only improved (My Agents, Events) is not his, round 2's rule from your dates; no card for a request with no page (Usage, Creator rewards, Build it Better for crypto communities, Airdrop Farmer while building) (A3).
The Supervisor in two layers (C). The engine layer, in the background: as your Telegram bot improved from its chats, it follows every company's chats with NEXUS, the tasks, their outcomes and what owners approved, rejected and complained about, and keeps improving the engine; owners never see it and it files nothing under their names. The company layer, in the Audit tab: a review of that company's processes, charters and setup that applies safe changes itself (each listed, undone with one click) and leaves what spends units or money, or adds or removes a department, to the owner as an approval; each finding links its message or task.
A countdown to the next pass in one place only, at the foot of the office first in the bar of the plan's windows: a cyan ring that empties, "next pass 0:12", "2/40 turns"; green "running now" while a turn runs; grey "paused" or "waiting for the window · 14:05"; from heartbeat.next_pass_at, pass_every_s and run_state.turns_today / turns_cap (E).
Play and pause per department: a round switch in its colour on its row (Command has none); its tasks wait, a running turn finishes, the others go on; the row says "Paused by you" or "Paused: the company is paused"; it survives a reload. A paused room goes dark in the office once its last turn ends (lamp, neon and floor light out, name dimmed, "Paused" on its wall); the whole company paused darkens every department, Command stays lit (F).
The revenue share (H) is a sketch only: how it really works is yours to decide (the shares, how many places are paid, the floor, the payout, what counts as revenue brought); we draw the look. Drawn: every company ranked by the revenue it brought Cymetica in 30 days, the top 3 sharing up to 5% of Cymetica's revenue; the mockup's 5% / 3% / 1%, $10,000 floor and monthly USDC are placeholders. Our one suggestion: never count revenue from the owner's own or linked accounts, and never pay a company more than it brought, so trading with yourself never pays.
