# Ready-to-paste texts for the directory forms that need a human (2026-10-10)

Why these are not sent by us: futuretools.io and the Moralis form carry a CAPTCHA, and AlternativeTo and SaaSHub need an account. Each is a two-minute paste. Facts below are from Cymetica's own pages (cymetica.com/llms.txt, /gem-screener/app, /build-it-better); check Pricing before pasting, it is the one field we could not verify.

## Gem Screener
- URL: https://cymetica.com/gem-screener/app
- Short description (futuretools, Moralis short): A research screen of tokenized crypto apps and chains, ranked by how cheap they are for the revenue they earn, with a supply-pressure view and a Degen meter per token.
- Long description: Gem Screener lists crypto apps and chains by market cap against annual revenue, compared with the sector, and adds a supply view (what can still be sold: unlocks, buybacks, staking), a Degen meter, hot sectors and an equal-weight Top Picks fund. Every figure carries its source. Free to read; AI agents can use it without a key (GET /api/v1/gem-screener/picks).
- Category: Finance (futuretools) / Research. Pricing: Free.

## Build it Better
- URL: https://cymetica.com/build-it-better
- Short description: Ask for a better version of any app and watch it built in the open on a public 24-hour clock.
- Long description: Build it Better takes a request for a new or improved app, turns it into a written spec you accept, and builds it on EventTrader with a public clock and a public board of requests and results. Requests are made through the page, the API or the MCP tools.
- Category: Productivity / Other. Pricing: check (a request costs a small unit; holders of 100+ ET10 are waived).

## Per place
- futuretools.io: form https://www.futuretools.io/submit-a-tool (name, tool name, URL, short description, category Finance, pricing, email, CAPTCHA). One submission per app.
- AlternativeTo: account with a verified email (a week old, per a secondary source), then "Suggest new application". They decline services and closed betas, and links inside descriptions: describe, do not link. Needs a square 280x280+ PNG/SVG logo.
- SaaSHub: account, add the product, verify the domain by email or an HTML tag.
- Moralis wiki: https://moralis.com/web3-wiki/ "Submit your own project". One project per form, so Supply Analyzer is its own submission (and the page does not say a second is allowed).
- Not for us: public-apis and public-api-lists take free public APIs, not apps, and reject marketing for paid services.
