# Review of the whole project, 2026-09-23

Asked for by the operator the same day: a complete in-depth review of the
project against their vision (docs/laborator-plan.md, "Pro tebe" and
Context). Six read-only readers ran in parallel: five on code defects, one
per area, and one architect (Fable) on whether the design realises the
vision at all. **Triaged the same day** by three `finding-triage` agents:
32 real, 3 minority, 2 dropped. The votes are at the end.

Baseline before the review: `pytest tests/meta tests/repo` 1119 passed, 1
failed (rule 12, the console's 06:48 poll not refolded; fixed in 796e8f53).

## The architect's verdict (design, not bugs)

**No.** It is a rigorous bug-bounty instrument with a laboratory bolted
on, and its self-maintenance is sized for a team while it runs on one Pro
plan. The vision is the inverse: the lab is the product and bugs are the
side quest.

- **The funnel:** 85 questions → 50 answers → 42 rooms → 4 verdicts → 0
  survived.
- **What the verdict reads:** their index history under an 80 % coverage
  floor, while our own paper P&L is continuous and ignored.
- **How rooms are keyed:** by Nexus answer, not by product.
- **How the budget is spent:** 27,843 Opus messages against 1,133 Sonnet.
- **Paperwork:** addressed to a reader who does not read (NEXT.md 42.7 KB,
  DEEP-PASS.md).
- **What adding a department costs:** 8–10 touchpoints.

Target, in short:
- **A department is one shape:** skill + `scripts/<d>.py` (tick · brief ·
  report) + `state/<d>*.jsonl` + `state/<d>/report.json`. Everything else
  discovers it by glob.
- **The lab tick ends in a `decide:` line.** Quiet ticks cost one short LLM
  turn.
- **The verdict comes from our paper book.**
- **One local `/lab` page,** not committed.
- **The deep pass is sized by the diff.**
- **CLAUDE.md ≤ 250 lines, one SessionStart hook, NEXT.md ≤ 10 open lines.**
- **Deletions:** finished docs and dead parts.

## Findings by area (claims until triaged)

Severity: **C** critical · **M** major · **m** minor.

### A — QA core (verdict: sound with defects)
- **A1 M** Folding an older run moves rows backwards. Nothing compares the run's `finished_at` with the ledger's `updated_at` (scripts/ledger.py:240-257, qa/state.py:429-497, qa/fold.py:294-318). A stale `--run` id can owe the vendor a false pass or fail.
- **A2 M** Rule 12 cannot catch a hand-edited check state after an `--events-only` fold (fold.py:791, ledger.py:372-388, state.py:380-395).
- **A3 M** A failing run rewrites which checks a row declares, because `checks` is copied from the failed results only (fold.py:849-852, findings.py:236). It has fired: CYM-documented-404 went 26 → 3 → 26.
- **A4 m** UNVERIFIABLE is entered with no transition (fold.py:227-252, 860-865). `_selector_is_full` counts `--deselect`, `--ignore` and `--lf` as full runs.
- **A5 m** Any `vendor_read` sets or clears the stale badge, including an a2a-inbox read clearing a tracker failure (fold.py:484-503).
- **A6 M** `protect_state.py` lets through absolute-path redirects, `cd state; >`, `Path.write_text`, raw-string `open(...,'a')` and `shutil.copy`. See also E7.
- **A7 m** A regression is recorded only from PASSING (fold.py:294).
- **A8 M** The fingerprint misses helpers, and `Measurement.fingerprint` replaces the code hash. `verify.py` then tells the vendor "not a rewritten one" falsely.

### B — outbound and safety (verdict: sound with defects)
- **B1 C** PROBE judges "inert" from the JSON body only. The query string, non-JSON bodies and MCP `{}` arguments pass (qa/safety.py:436-444, 317-325). Example: `POST .../toggle-trading?enabled=true` reaches the transport at PROBE.
- **B2 M** A `fail` verification from an R-level run goes out under V-level wording (verify.py:158-159, 373-376). Never fired.
- **B3 M** A withdrawn finding whose check passes still owes a pass verification (fold.py:740-742). #54 and #108 are one pin away.
- **B4 M** Filing events carry no `run_id`/`finding_id`, so what a report promises "on request" is not kept (submit.py:294-335, keepset.py:189-196).
- **B5 m** `submit.py` composes a report whatever the check's current state is. Rule 10 is prose only.
- **B6 m** An outcome accepted without evidence becomes permanently invisible to the fold and to `--reconcile`.
- **B7 m** `cloned_bots.settings` PATCH has no paper floor anywhere.

### C — laboratory and trading numbers (verdict: sound with defects; paper-only holds everywhere)
- **C1 C** A failed benchmark read becomes `[]`, and the verdict then judges on return alone: refuted turns into survived. It is written once and never re-judged (qa/lab.py:1048-1071, scripts/lab.py:891-899).
- **C2 M** `board` counts stake for every buy but P&L only for positions the sampler read. Closed positions' realized P&L is ignored, so the ranking sent to Nexus is wrong (qa/lab.py:1527-1569).
- **C3 M** Only an early verdict closes card positions. At a normal window close the position stays open and counted (scripts/lab.py:1202-1209). **First case: STR-0006, 2026-09-26 00:52 UTC.**
- **C4 M** n_eff pairs returns by list position, not by time, so ρ ≈ 0 and the 0.6 warning can never fire (scripts/lab.py:912-966).
- **C5 M** The memecoin hot-vs-control `compare()` has no pair-switch filter, compares different periods, and counts per signal rather than per token. The stored 13.1 % vs 7.4 % is 6.3 % vs 1.4 % clean, dominated by one token (qa/fomo.py:687-758).
- **C6 M** Hot headlines drop time exits without a round in ±30 min, so the sample is loaded with extreme exits; `control()` is always n = 0 (qa/hot.py:527-551).
- **C7 M** The Nexus answer parser reads comparison prose as picks (NEX-0080 → 5 bots) (qa/lab.py:1304-1318).
- **C8 m** Drawdown is measured in percentage points of return, not from the peak (qa/lab.py:900-914, 1036-1046).
- **C9 m** Two writers on samples.jsonl; `_next_id` is taken outside the lock (paper.py:492, clone.py:295).

### D — console and data (verdict: sound with defects)
- **D1 M** The pruner deletes captures the manifest knows before any archive has bundled them. 392 are lost for good (prune_runs.py:282-347, keep.py:110-128).
- **D2 M** The page hash covers inputs no commit carries: gitignored diaries and working copies (render.py:404-406, pre-commit:51). A fresh clone fails rule 12.
- **D3 m** `/`, `/payload.json`, `/events` and `/status` are served without the Host check `/rooms.json` has (serve.py:531-558).
- **D4 m** The FOMO diary snapshot adds about 1 MB a day to git (46 % of pack growth). The 150 MB ceiling trips in about a month (keep.py:45-54).
- **D5 m** A rebuilt bundle is never compared with the old one. A thinned manifest would silently drop 446 archived-only captures.
- **D6 m** The vendor's a2a messages are listed as kept, but their captures are not in `must_keep`.

### E — self-maintenance (verdict: sound with defects)
- **E1 M** The reminders do not reach the operator:
  - 4 of 24 hook runs timed out and printed nothing;
  - the surface scan grows with evidence;
  - drift crowds out the important lines (LIMIT = 4);
  - sessions repeated the reminder in only 2–3 of 9 starts.
- **E2 M** Memory bodies are not read. Only 9 of 50 reads were full, and the deep pass's step 0 read titles only (46–69 KB per class).
- **E3 M** The harness loads only the first 200 lines / 25,000 characters of MEMORY.md, so new memories start being cut in about three weeks.
- **E4 M** The patrol's idle flag cannot fire for 34 of 47 parts. Slash commands and `.md` preregistrations are invisible, so "nothing idle" is reported as health.
- **E5 M** A pass that dies halfway is silent. Its confirmed findings are overwritten by the next step 0, and the lab stays blocked for 24 h.
- **E6 m** Step 1's readers are mostly `/loop` noise, and `trail` truncates to 120 entries silently.
- **E7 m** Same as A6.
- **E8 m** Rows in `audits/processes.jsonl` are 7-day totals labelled daily.
