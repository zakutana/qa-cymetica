What these numbers mean

Reachability is the share of each section's documented endpoints that answer correctly right now, measured by read-only GET at two identities (anonymous and TRUSTED). A 401 counts as healthy — the gate doing its job is not a defect. A 404 on an endpoint the platform documents does not.

Checks is how many real assertions exist about that section. It is deliberately a separate number: a section can be 100% reachable and entirely unverified, and merging the two would hide exactly the gap worth acting on.

Not established by this page: that reachable endpoints return correct data, that gated endpoints are gated for the right reasons, or that any documented behaviour matches what actually happens when called. Those need checks, and most sections have none yet.
