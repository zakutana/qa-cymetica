Cymetica QA Console

Independent verification of the Cymetica / EventTrader API, MCP and discovery surfaces. Every number below was measured, not reported.

Findings

Everything found so far, including defects in the platform's own documentation. Open one to read the exact correspondence sent about it. Nothing here is marked fixed — only verified against a named surface, at a named time, by a named check.

How to read this page

The big percentage answers one question: of all the addresses Cymetica publishes for this part of the platform, how many actually answer when you call them?

We call each address twice — once with no key, once with the API key — and take the better of the two answers. Then each address lands in one of these:

It works. Counts as good.
“You need to log in.” The lock is doing its job. Counts as good.
“You forgot something.” It is there, it just wants a parameter. Counts as good.
“No such thing here.” They wrote it in their own docs but it does not exist. Counts as bad.
“Wrong way to ask.” Our mistake, not theirs. Left out of the score entirely.
Cannot be checked this way. Live feeds, or addresses needing an ID we do not have. Left out.

Worked example — Markets & prices, 64%. It publishes 16 addresses. Five could not be checked with a simple call, leaving 11. Of those, seven answered properly and four said “no such thing here”. Seven out of eleven is 64%.

“Checks” is a completely different number, and most cards say no checks yet. It counts the real tests written for that part. An address answering does not mean the answer is correct — 100% with no checks means “it is alive, and nobody has verified a word it says”. Those two things are kept apart on purpose: merging them would hide the gap that is actually worth acting on.

So this page does not tell you whether the numbers Cymetica returns are right, whether the locks are on the right doors, or whether anything behaves the way they documented. That is what the checks are for, and that is the work still ahead.
